Hi. This was written by a real person on paper with pen before making its way to machine. One person (soon many, hopefully) building AI tools for people, by people. I've never been the smartest person, but I made up for it by always rotating under the 5 Ps: proper preparation prevents poor performance. I relate to open weight models in that way, so I built this: a tool to situate very capable agents who just need the right environment to thrive.

The architecture works like this: every Sovereign instance brings open weight intelligence situated within enriched knowledge, completely in your network. That's fine for most, but I hope some will have that midnight yearning and wonder: "Could I run MiniMax2.7... locally?" Then walk next door to their neighbor who is famous for their beefy gaming machine and ask humbly if they would join a Commonwealth. Each Sovereign gains compute by joining a Commonwealth. That's what I want the future of AI to look like. You're here at the beginning and not everything will work at first. Be patient, be constructive, then when this shit actually works demand the best. That's all I can ask.

Commonwealth lets a few people you trust pool their machines to run larger models together. By invitation and always on your terms.

We'll email you exactly once, when the desktop build and cli is ready to download. No drip. No newsletter.

We don't know how this ends. We know why we started, and we know who we started for, and we know that the work is worth it for that reason alone.
