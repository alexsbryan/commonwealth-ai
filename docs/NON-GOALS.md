# What this isn't trying to be

A project is shaped as much by what it turns down. If your idea lands on one of
these lines, it's not a bad idea — it's just not this project. Saves us both a
long thread.

- **It won't quietly send your data anywhere.** No telemetry, no usage stats, no
  crash reports that leave your machine on their own. If a feature only works by
  shipping your stuff somewhere, the answer is no. That's the whole point.
- **It isn't a hosted service.** No cloud to log into, no account, no server we
  run for you. The model answering you lives on your machine. Want something
  managed? Wrong tool, and that's fine.
- **It isn't chasing the leaderboard.** Honest, grounded, traceable on hardware
  you own — over a flashier benchmark score that trades those away.
- **It isn't an everything-platform.** Workflows and connected tools, yes. An
  app store / plugin economy to police, no.
- **It won't grow a knob for every preference.** Good defaults beat a wall of
  toggles. We add a setting when real use shows the default's wrong — not for a
  case of one.

**Where the lines bend:** a new *local, cited* data source; a platform we don't
cover yet (if you can help test); a default a lot of people independently work
around. The test is always the same — your data stays yours, your machine does
the work, every answer traces to something real. Hold that line and it's
welcome.
