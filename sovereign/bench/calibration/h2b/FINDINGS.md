# H2b — the evidence counterfactual, measured at 36B. Verdict: no Phase-2 standing on this supply.

**BLUF.** On 4,207 pairs decoded by the production-class 36B with the daemon
stopped, the evidence-ablation counterfactual **does not earn Phase-2
standing**, and the result is robust to every reading of the data:

- **Formally: `CouldNotJudge`** — the pre-registered stop condition fired. Arm
  B refused on **80.9%** of absent pairs against the order's 80% bar, so the
  dependence statistic is partially collapsed toward refusal-detection.
- **Substantively: both kill clauses fail anyway.** Computed on the full
  held-out set regardless of the stop — n = **1,926** non-leaked pairs, large
  enough that no interval caveat applies — evidence dependence adds
  **+0.0010 AUROC** over the H1 rerank margin (0.9054 vs 0.9044; clause A
  requires +0.02), and dependence alone reaches **0.6234** (clause B requires
  0.85). There is **no reading of this data under which H2b clears its bar.**

The stop condition being marginal (0.9 points past 80%) therefore does not
matter: waive it and the verdict becomes `Killed` by both clauses instead of
`CouldNotJudge` by the stop. Same standing either way — none.

**The operator's framing, which the data supports:** this is *"no standing on
THIS supply"*, not *"the mechanism is dead"*. The label supply is generated by
our own ingestion pipeline and is fixable; the two named re-open paths are in
§6. But §5 adds a sobering prior for the fast-follow: the number a fixed supply
must beat is **0.9044 from a 0.6B reranker that runs in milliseconds** — and on
the one unit this supply *could* answer, three decodes of a 36B added one part
in a thousand.

## 1. What was run

2026-08-09, BeefyMac (M2 Max, 64 GB), daemon **stopped** for the entire decode
window (verified unreachable before launch; restarted only after the last row).
Generator **FINAL-Bench_Darwin-36B-Opus-Q6_K** — the engine of record. Greedy,
seeds pinned, three arms per pair:

| arm | prompt | feeds |
|---|---|---|
| A | question + full evidence pool | dependence (vs B), the P2/P3 statistics |
| B | identical prompt, chunks ablated | dependence — **the order's counterfactual, verbatim** |
| P | question, no evidence frame at all | `parametric_known` (the leak detector) and nothing else |

Two decode units (D2's finding, committed 224a7bbd, `D2_SHAKEOUT.md`): the
spec's **value** unit is structurally unanswerable by this supply — all 4,207
questions are "Is it true that ...?" propositions with no value to extract — so
the decision-grade run used the **verdict** unit (YES/NO/NONE), with a 100-pair
value-unit control to test whether the collapse was a 4B artefact.

| leg | unit | n | exit |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary | verdict | 4,207 (the full calibration set) | 0 |
| control | value | 100 | 0 |
| smoke, T=0.7 + T=5.0 discriminator | (k-sample) | 5 probes x k=5 x 2 | 3, 1 |

Measured cost: **0.49 pairs/s** (2.0 s/pair for three arms) — 10x under the
19-31 s/pair estimate; the Darwin-36B is MoE with a small active set. Full set:
1h49m. Determinism: **169 arm-A re-decodes, 0 unstable.** 0 garbage rows in
4,207 (the 36B always terminated inside the 24-token budget).

## 2. The primary, in numbers

Census: 2,126 calibration / 2,081 holdout; 2,255 answerable / 1,952 absent.

| gate stage | result | bar | outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 leak rate (arm P value present in the withheld passage) | 296/2,255 = **13.1%** | predicted 10-40% at 36B | **within band** — the leak detector works, and the exclusion it drives is real |
| Arm-B refusal on absent | **80.9%** | stop at 80% | **STOP fired** |
| P2 mean dependence, answerable vs absent | 0.7929 vs 0.5461 = **+0.2468** | >= 0.15 | clears |
| P3 clause A: combined - margin AUROC | 0.9054 - 0.9044 = **+0.0010** | >= +0.02 | **fails** |
| P3 clause B: dependence AUROC, orthogonality | **0.6234**, r = 0.3393 | >= 0.85 with \|r\| < 0.5 | **fails** |

The shape of the failure is informative. P2 clears: dependence *is* a real
phenomenon on this data — arm A changes its answer when the evidence vanishes
more often on answerable pairs than absent ones. But P3 shows the signal it
carries is almost entirely the signal the H1 margin already carries (r = 0.34,
fitted combination weight w = 0.5, gain +0.0010). The counterfactual is a
slower, noisier read of a quantity the reranker margin measures in
milliseconds. The naive ceilings (0.4990 / 0.5010 on a near-balanced holdout)
confirm neither class dominates — the 0.9044 margin AUROC is genuine
discrimination, not class imbalance.

## 3. The control: the value-unit collapse is the supply's, not the model's

100 pairs, unit = value, same 36B: **arm B refused on 100 of 100** — exactly
the 4B's collapse, reproduced on a model 9x larger. "Is it true that X?" minus
evidence draws `NONE` from every model we have tried, at every scale, because
the prompt's own escape hatch ("if the EVIDENCE does not state it, reply
NONE") is satisfied by an empty evidence block on every pair. The value unit is
dead **on this supply** — settled at both scales, and the reason is the
supply's question form, not model capacity.

The control also shows what D2 predicted the abstention-detector artifact looks
like at 36B: with arm B constant, the gate computes dependence AUROC 0.8077 and
a clause-A-passing combined gain of +0.0535 — numbers that would read as a win,
generated by a statistic that has collapsed to "did arm A assert anything".
The stop condition discards them, which is its job. Value-unit P1 also
collapses honestly: leak 1/54 = 1.9%, because arm P refuses 81/100 — the 36B
will not *produce* a specific value unprompted by evidence, even where §2 shows
it will *verify* one (arm P asserted on 4,092/4,207 verdict-unit rows). Leak
detection needs the verdict form on this supply too.

## 4. The smoke: spec Appendix A stays closed

The h2/FINDINGS.md §5.B1 question — "if the 36B's draws are also 1/5 distinct
at T<=1.0, H2 is dead on this unit regardless of any bank" — is answered:
**1/5 distinct on every value-asserting probe at T=0.7, reproducibility
proven.** The T=5.0 discriminator drew 5/5 distinct on all probes, clearing
both the sampler and the k-fanout (including the recurrent-layer KV concern —
Darwin-36B is Qwen3.5-family) of blame: the T=0.7 degeneracy is a genuinely
peaked posterior. k-sample entropy has nothing to measure on this unit at
production scale. Its exit 1 is the honest could-not-judge for the
deliberately skipped repro half at T=5.0 (proven minutes earlier at T=0.7).

## 5. What this means for the initiative

1. **H1's margin stands as the answerability signal.** 0.9044 held-out AUROC
   from the 0.6B reranker, at ~ms cost. H2b spent three 36B decodes per pair to
   add +0.0010 to it.
2. **H2 (entropy) and H2b (counterfactual) are now both resolved against
   Phase-2 on the current units and supply** — H2 by a degenerate posterior at
   both scales, H2b by redundancy with the margin plus a partially collapsed
   statistic.
3. **The fast-follow (value-form question generation, seat todo c0bda2ab)
   should carry a falsifiable target, stated now:** on a value-form supply, the
   verdict-unit run's numbers are the prior to beat — dependence must clear
   0.85 AUROC alone or add >= +0.02 over the margin, same bars, same split
   discipline. If extraction-form questions do not materially move dependence
   away from the margin's signal (prediction to falsify: they will not move
   clause A past +0.02), the mechanism should be retired rather than re-run.

## 6. The two named re-open paths

- **Supply:** value-form questions from the ingestion pipeline (the fix is
  scheduled; we control the generator). This re-opens the *value unit* and
  gives the leak detector its natural form.
- **Behaviour:** the 80.9% refusal-collapse names the 36B's abstention training
  under an empty EVIDENCE block. A generator that asserts more under ablation
  would decollapse the statistic — but note the 4B (19.6% refusal on absent)
  already provided that condition in D2, and its dependence AUROC was 0.4731.
  Neither endpoint of the abstention spectrum has yet shown the mechanism
  beating the margin.
