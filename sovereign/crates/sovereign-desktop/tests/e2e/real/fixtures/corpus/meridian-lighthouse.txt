# The Meridian Lighthouse

The Meridian Lighthouse stands on the basalt headland of Cape Vesper,
overlooking the Strait of Hollow Sound. Construction began in 1885 and
the light was first lit on 14 March 1887. The tower is 47 meters tall
from base to lantern gallery, making it the tallest structure on the
cape.

The lighthouse was staffed continuously by resident keepers from 1887
until 1974, when the light was automated and the last keeper departed.
After automation, the keeper's cottage was converted into a small
maritime museum operated by the Cape Vesper Historical Society.

The light's characteristic signal is one white flash every 18 seconds,
visible for 21 nautical miles in clear weather. Mariners know it
informally as "the Meridian wink."
