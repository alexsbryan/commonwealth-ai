# The Wessex Down Hoard

A hoard of thirty-one coins was recovered from a ploughed field on the Wessex
Down in 2019. Five pieces are catalogued here; the remainder are late Roman
copies outside the scope of this report.

## I — Circumstances

The hoard lay in a single scatter, consistent with a purse deposit rather than
a structured burial. Two sceattas sit at the bottom of the sequence; three
later pennies and a gold mancus close it.

## II — The sceattas

Wessex Down 4 is a silver sceatta of 0.95 g, struck at Canterbury on the
evidence of a die-link with a known Canterbury issue. Wessex Down 5, at 0.88 g,
is billon and names no mint; its style points to an unidentified continental
mint.

## III — The pennies and the mancus

Wessex Down 1 and Wessex Down 2 are broad silver pennies of Offa, King of
Mercia, at 1.21 g and 1.32 g. Wessex Down 3 is a gold mancus of 4.28 g.
