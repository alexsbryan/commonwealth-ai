# Catalogue and attributions

Attributions below follow Michael Metcalf's classification. Each entry states
the coin, the mint, and the date range the attribution proposes, with the
grade of evidence it rests on.

## Marlow Field 1

Struck for Offa at London, 757-796. The reverse names the moneyer; the obverse
portrait places it in the second phase of the broad penny coinage.

## Marlow Field 4

Struck at Canterbury between 710 and 725. The attribution is die-linked to a
Canterbury issue in the Aston Rowant hoard, which is the strongest grade of
evidence available for a sceatta of this series.
