# The Saltgrass Ledger — outline + fact ledger (bank source of truth)

Corpus id: chaos-saltgrass. 12 chapters, ~14k words. All proper nouns invented
(checked: no collisions with famous fiction — "Quoyle" avoided deliberately).

## World
- Village: **Glasswater Stave**, on the **Hethering coast**. Tidal lock basin
  connects the inner harbor to the sea.
- Inn: **The Cold Lantern**, kept by **Widow Hetch** (first name NEVER given).
- Lighthouse: **Wrack Point**, kept by **Lessa Pellow**.
- Era: vaguely early-motor age; the YEAR IS NEVER STATED (absent by design).

## Cast
- **Maren Ashquill** — tide-registrar; keeps the official tide ledger. Narrator-adjacent protagonist. Age NEVER stated.
- **Corwin Pellow** — harbormaster, the victim. Found drowned in the lock basin.
- **Severin Quenholt** — salvage broker, the killer/forger. His lugger is NEVER named (absent by design).
- **Tabb Orrison** — lock-keeper's apprentice; FINDS the body at dawn.
- **Doctor Imbrey Fosk** — coroner; verdict: drowning, with a head wound got BEFORE the water.
- **Constable Marlock** — first name NEVER given (absent by design).
- **Lessa Pellow** — the victim's sister, lighthouse keeper at Wrack Point.

## Plot facts (PRESENT questions)
1. Killer: **Severin Quenholt**.
2. Weapon: a **boat-hook** (strikes Pellow; the flooding lock does the rest).
3. The boat-hook belonged to the **Merrow Bell's salvage gear** (NOT Quenholt's own lugger — distractor).
4. Stolen object: the **Lyle-Hannett chronometer** from the harbor office.
5. Hidden: inside a **salt barrel** in the **net-loft**.
6. Forged: the tide ledger's **night page for the fourteenth**.
7. Forgery exposed by ink: the office uses **iron-gall ink**; the forged line is in **logwood ink**.
8. Tide that night: a **neap tide** — the lock could not have self-flooded; someone opened the sluice.
9. Body found by: **Tabb Orrison**, at dawn, in the lock basin.
10. Motive: the **Merrow Bell** salvage claim — a sunken ketch whose insured
    cargo Quenholt had already quietly stripped and sold; Pellow's survey would
    have exposed the empty hold.
11. Pellow's last ledger entry: "**Sluice gate ironwork wants seeing to.**"
12. Maren's office/role: **tide-registrar**.
13. Lessa Pellow's occupation: lighthouse keeper (Wrack Point).
14. Doctor's verdict: drowning + head wound before the water (Fosk).
15. Quenholt confesses when confronted with the **salt crystals** on the
    chronometer's case matching the net-loft barrel + the logwood ink.
16. The chronometer's tell: its **broken winding-crown** (snapped in the struggle) —
    found in the lock basin's silt grate.
17. Maximal: how the forgery was exposed (ink + neap tide + winding-crown chain).
18. Maximal: what the Merrow Bell affair reveals about the village's salvage economy.
19. Maximal: Maren's method — ledgers as instruments of truth (theme).
