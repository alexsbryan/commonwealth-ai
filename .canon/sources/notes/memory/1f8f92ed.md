# Prompts (app AND measurement/judge) must be succinct and absolutely non-contradictory — the models are smaller open-weight

Prompts (app AND measurement/judge) must be succinct and absolutely non-contradictory — the models are smaller open-weight

When writing ANY prompt in this project — the app's synthesis/gate/scan prompts AND offline judge/oracle prompts (e.g. rejudge-length-blind.mjs, scan_unsupported_specifics) — keep it SUCCINCT and ABSOLUTELY NON-CONTRADICTORY.

Why: the SUT and the judges run smaller open-weight models (Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTP and below). Long, multi-rule, or subtly-conflicting instructions degrade them — they follow the last/loudest clause, conflate scoped rules, or judge inconsistently. This is different from steering a frontier model, which tolerates nuance.

How to apply: prefer a short ORDERED decision procedure over a list of parallel rules that could overlap. "Decide in order, stop at the first match: 1)… 2)… 3)…" makes scoped exceptions (e.g. "labeled general-knowledge is honest" checked BEFORE "unsourced specific = fabrication") structurally non-contradictory instead of relying on the model to reconcile them. Cut restatements and adjectives. When a rubric grows several nuanced clauses, that is the smell — compress it.

Concrete instance (2026-07-01): the length-blind re-judge rubric had grown to ~15 lines of parallel rules (flag unsourced specifics / labeled-GK is honest / honest-limitation is good) that a 35B judge could read as conflicting. Rewrote it as a 3-step ordered decision (honest-decline → broken → else good). See [[project_chaos_evidence_capture_artifact_2026_07_01]].
