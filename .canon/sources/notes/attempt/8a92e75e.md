Speculative decoding (classic draft, llama-server) on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B Primary target on Strix Halo: net-negative (0.87× wall-time vs baseline 45.82 tok/s). A3B-class MoE active-parameter math (3B active per token) makes target decode so cheap that draft+verify overhead always wins. Not a bandwidth question; not solvable by switching engines within the same family. Prior research warned this for consumer Ampere; measurement confirms on Strix Halo UMA too.
