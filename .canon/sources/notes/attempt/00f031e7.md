# Widening candidates_k alone (without atlas signal) does NOT improve SEP retrieval. Sweep: k=50 → 51/66 sources, k=500 → 51/66 sources.…

Widening candidates_k alone (without atlas signal) does NOT improve SEP retrieval. Sweep: k=50 → 51/66 sources, k=500 → 51/66 sources. Cross-encoder over-promotes tangential-but-dense articles at scale; per-article dedup then fills top-10 with those. Canonical articles ARE in the wide pool (60/66 sources at k=500 limit=50) but lose the dedup tiebreak. Either wider output limit or different tiebreaker needed — atlas turned out to be both.
