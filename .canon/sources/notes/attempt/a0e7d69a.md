THE CLAIM-SEARCH LADDER IS LOSSY — IT DESTROYS 23% OF RESCUES FOR 6.8% WALL. MEASURED 2026-08-05, DO NOT FLIP `SOVEREIGN_GATE_CLAIM_SEARCH_LADDER` ON.

THE NUMBERS (78 claims, `sep-summarize-slowtail` scratch bank, SHADOW=1 + LADDER=0 — the only configuration where a skipped claim's rescue is still observable, because with the ladder ON a skipped claim is never searched and its rescue is unobservable by construction):
  real rescues (calibrated)          26  (33% of claims)
  claims the ladder would SKIP       41  (53% — this is the fan-out it saves)
  LOST RESCUES (skipped AND rescued)  6  = 23% of all real rescues
  batch-vs-calibrated agreement      67/78 = 86%
  newly_failed                        2
The first claim of the run was a lost rescue: vp_chunks_only=0.952 vs vp_production=0.762 at tau=0.900, triage=Some(true).

WHY THE SAFETY ARGUMENT FAILED. It claimed losslessness BY CONSTRUCTION: a rescue is by definition a claim that fails without re-search and passes with it, so every rescue has stage-1 vp >= tau and always reaches stage 2. That is sound ONLY while stage 1 is the CALIBRATED per-claim forced-choice. It is not — stage 1 is `claims_support_batched`, a text A/B whose tau semantics differ from the calibrated logit. Two instruments, so their agreement is an empirical property of the sample. The 14% disagreement is not spread evenly; it lands exactly on the claims that needed the search.

THE TRAP THIS ILLUSTRATES: the withdrawal lived only in a session frame while the CODE still asserted the guarantee in two places (`grounding/mod.rs` "LOSSLESS FOR RESCUES BY CONSTRUCTION — a property of the definition, not of the sample" and `grounding/config.rs` "The ladder cannot drop one"). A future session reading either comment would have flipped the flag on a guarantee its author had already retracted. Both now state the narrower position and name the promotion gate. WITHDRAWING A CLAIM MEANS EDITING THE CODE THAT MAKES IT, not just the frame.

BOTH KNOWN STAGE-1 INSTRUMENTS NOW FAIL: calibrated per-claim stage 1 = net-NEGATIVE +5.0s wall (note a4be8afd — a restored pinned prefix does not make a forced-choice free); batched stage 1 = fast but lossy (this note). A re-open needs a THIRD instrument, not a retuned threshold.

THE INSTRUMENT IS KEPT AND IS THE RE-OPEN GATE. `claim_search_shadow` now carries `triage`, `ladder_would_skip`, `triage_agrees`, `lost_rescue`. Promotion condition: `lost_rescue == 0` summed over a bank. `batched_support` is now also populated when claim-search shadow is on, purely so the triage verdict can be logged with the ladder off.

CAVEAT ON STRENGTH: claim extraction is non-deterministic run to run, so 23% is one run's RATE. The EXISTENCE of the failure mode is settled — 6 independent instances — and no plausible rate makes the trade good, since the gate is lost_rescue == 0.
