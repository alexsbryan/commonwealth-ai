REVERTED — an aboutness gate on multi-source expansion measured ZERO impact. Built and measured 2026-08-05 on `bench sep/summarize --prod-pipeline`, then reverted per "complexity that doesn't move a named metric gets reverted, not defended".

WHAT WAS TRIED. `expand_from_top_sources` ranks `(corpus_id, title)` groups by best score, takes the top `EXPANSION_MULTI_SOURCE_GROUPS`, and tops each up to `EXPANSION_MULTI_PER_SOURCE` — with NO test of whether the winning group is about the question. It already computed query-token overlap but used it only to ORDER candidates INSIDE a chosen group (`group_chunks.sort_by_key`), never for admission — the same "relevance orders but never admits" shape as audit D1. I added a >0 query-token-overlap bar on the group.

RESULT: identical on every number. off-topic 46/420=11.0%, personal 31, score-1.0 chunks 17, sep 192, wiki 182, fact 0.7500/0.7833 — byte-for-byte the same as the arm without it. The gate fired twice in 14 questions and correctly refused `enron-sample-multi-wide::God Bless You` and `::GOD BLESS` on a question about the cosmological argument for the existence of god (they share ZERO query tokens — `god` is 3 chars, below `EVIDENCE_TITLE_MIN_TOKEN_LEN`, so those groups won on embedding proximity alone). Those refusals were real, and they were not reaching the final 30 anyway.

WHY A TOKEN BAR CANNOT WORK HERE, measured offline against the report snippets. Framing-stripped topic-token overlap for the amplified chunks:
  obsidian::Improve Sep Skeleton                      4 tokens
  conversations-anthropic::Joan Robinson's economic…  1-2
  commonwealth-ai::PhilosophyDomain                   0-1
  enron::God Bless You                                0
`Improve Sep Skeleton` is a personal note ABOUT THE SEP CORPUS, so it genuinely shares vocabulary with a SEP question. No token-count threshold separates "a note about the SEP project" from "a SEP article about idealism". The signal that DOES separate them is the reweighted score — ~0.70 vs ~0.04, about 17x — and expansion never sees it.

THE ACTUAL DEFECT IS ORDERING, NOT A MISSING PREDICATE. Expansion (like atom_enum, note c9aa59c6) selects its groups at a pipeline point where the pool is RRF-fused and everything sits in 0.02-0.05, a band where grounded hits and hopeless ones are indistinguishable. Adding predicates downstream of a bad ranking cannot recover what the ranking lost. Do not re-attempt a per-group relevance bar; fix the position.

ALSO RULED OUT: `expand_from_dominant_source` is NOT the source of the score-1.0 runs on this bank — it never ran (it is gated on the FastFocused route + `top_source_repeat_count >= 2`, and these turns route DeepQuery/REASONING). The 17 score-1.0 chunks come from `expand_from_top_sources`' `fetch_chunks_by_title` top-ups, which carry no query score. Confirmed from the trace: `multi-source expansion (additive)` fires 14 times, dominant-source zero.
