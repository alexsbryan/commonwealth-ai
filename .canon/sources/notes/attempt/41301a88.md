SOLID
- The memory is ANONYMOUS: 18.80 GB [anon], 1.96 GB [heap], 0.09 GB binary (from /proc/<pid>/smaps aggregated by backing). NOT GPU, NOT model mmap — /dev/dri/renderD128 and the GGUF are large VIRTUAL, ~zero RESIDENT. Never read sizes from /proc/<pid>/maps; they are virtual and will make you blame unified memory on Strix Halo.
- ~90 anon mappings are exactly 64/128 MiB AND 64 MiB-aligned = the glibc HEAP_MAX_SIZE signature, about 8 GB.
- Only ~3.3 GB is attributable to currently-resident models: two 4B (Qwen3.5-4B-UD-MTP-Q6_K_XL, Qwopus3.5-4B-v3-MTP-Q8_0). Everything 35B is `loaded=False residency=cold`.
- No `#[global_allocator]`, `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX` unset, `max_blocking_threads` absent workspace-wide (tokio default 512), 61 `spawn_blocking` sites reachable.
- RSS OSCILLATES 22.1-23.5 GB. It does not trend at idle (200 s watch: threads 49, arenas 85, RSS ended 75 MB LOWER) and does not grow under any read-only load I could drive.

RETRACTED — THREE THINGS I GOT WRONG
1. "~15 GB is arenas" — inferred from a resident-size histogram. Direct counting gives ~8 GB.
2. "The uncapped blocking pool bursts and creates arenas that outlive their threads" — NOT SUPPORTED, and now actively contradicted: under 16 CONCURRENT heavy requests (16/16 HTTP 200, ~3.5 s and 1.5 MB each) the thread count NEVER MOVED off 49 and arenas moved +2. The blocking pool does not expand under concurrency here, so it cannot be the arena factory.
3. "+1.4 GB from the atlas probing" — noise. RSS swings ±700 MB all session; I read a trend into a band.

THE SIX FAILED DRIVERS, so nobody repeats them
1. 12x CLI `tools call code_search` — output sent to /dev/null, never proved it reached the daemon (the CLI may run ToolRegistry in-process). INVALID.
2. 16x `corpus_search` over /mcp — 6/16 replies, all "requires `embedding`"; sampler killed by my own `wait` at 47 samples. INVALID twice.
3. 12x /v1/chat/completions — only 1/12 returned; the daemon SERIALIZES inference on its fast slot, so concurrency stayed in the curl clients. Not a test.

Background samplers died repeatedly (setsid PGID handling); before/after snapshots are the reliable instrument, not a sampler.

BEST REMAINING HYPOTHESIS, UNPROVEN: a big model loaded and evicted earlier in this process's life, its buffers freed to the allocator but never returned to the OS. `commonwealth/primary` is advertised and targets Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-MTP-UD-Q6_K, 28.0 GB on disk, currently cold. That fits everything — huge anon, flat under all load, plateau reached early, VmHWM 24.8 GB, restart-only recovery — and it explains why no read-only workload reproduces it.

WHY I COULD NOT PROVE IT — AND THE THING TO FIX FIRST. This daemon's fd 1 and 2 are PIPES to its parent (2008467). There is no log file for the running process. `~/.svrnmesh/daemon.log` is stale from MAY 19 and belongs to a long-dead process; its 35B entries are four months old and prove nothing about this one. So there is NO HISTORY to diagnose from, which is exactly why this problem "recurs" — every occurrence is undiagnosable after the fact.
