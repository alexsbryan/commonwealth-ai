RUN-CHANNEL CASE LAW (2026-08-19, second incident of the class): a systemd-run battery wrapper that ends with a trailing `echo` masks the inner command's non-zero exit — the t6b one-shot leg ran `cargo test` from the wrong cwd (home dir), exited 101, the wrapper's final echo exited 0, and the battery reported clean. First incident: the ARMS_RUN_ROOT zero-flight launch (same mask shape). Rule for every future battery wrapper: propagate the inner exit code (set -euo pipefail, or explicit `exit $?` after the payload, NEVER a trailing echo last), and emit a per-leg exit marker the scorer's completeness guard can see. The worker caught both and relaunched (t6b-oneshot-re with --working-directory set) — but the next incident may not be caught by the worker; make it structural in the wrapper template.
