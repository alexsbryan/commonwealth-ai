A CAUGHT VERDICT IS NOT A NEW REQUIREMENT, AND THE GR FAMILY'S REAL MOVEMENT IS 17 -> 20. Measured 2026-09-01 after the first clean adjudication.

THE TRAP. The adjudicator returned 7 CAUGHT and it is tempting to read that as +7. Four of the seven — GR-17, GR-19, GR-22, GR-28 — were ALREADY in the claimed set, which is exactly the 17 the campaign's "17 of 625" counts. Only GR-18, GR-47 and GR-51 are new. Always difference the CAUGHT set against quality/conformance/*.toml before quoting a number; `scripts/mint-claims.py` does this and reports mint/skip/REFUSE per row.

THE FOUR RE-PROVEN ONES ARE NOT WASTE. They were `covers:` tags asserted by whoever wrote them, and 6 of 13 such tags were overclaims on adversarial review (note cf566968). They now have mutation-earned proof. That is a quality upgrade to the existing 17, not a count.

THE LAST MILE HAS TWO STEPS AFTER MINTING AND NEITHER IS OPTIONAL:
  1. UPDATE_CONFORMANCE_TAGS=1 cargo test -p kernel-types --test conformance_tags
  2. a FULL suite run.
`svrn conformance` refuses a claim whose source file changed after the report was written — and ADDING THE TAG IS THAT CHANGE. All three new claims rendered `could-not-judge` with "passed, but <file> changed since that run" until the suite is re-run. A FILTERED re-run is worse than none: the report it writes contains only the tests it ran, so every other claim in the repo becomes never-ran.

`mint-claims.py` REFUSES rather than overwrites when a requirement is already claimed by a DIFFERENT test (GR-17 and GR-19 hit this). A second independent proof of the same clause is a decision for a person; a script that silently retargets a claim is how a good claim gets replaced by a worse one.

PROJECTION, STATED AS A PROJECTION. GR yielded 7 CAUGHT / 62 candidates / 50 requirements = ~14% of requirements reached. Held across all 582 command-settleable requirements that is ~80 CAUGHT, and since only 17 are claimed today nearly all would be new: roughly 17 -> 90. That is a 5x move and it is NOT the campaign's 300+. GR is also the family with the most existing claims (11 of the 17), so families like FE (133 requirements, ZERO claims) may convert better. Do not quote 300 until a family that is not GR has been adjudicated.
