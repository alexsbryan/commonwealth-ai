# RECALL MAY BE LOOSE; THE LABEL MAY NOT. The split that makes the next-edit golden set trustworthy, settled 2026-08-05…

RECALL MAY BE LOOSE; THE LABEL MAY NOT. The split that makes the next-edit golden set trustworthy, settled 2026-08-05 (`gym/next-edit/golden/precision.py`).

THE QUESTION: the shape detectors in `shapes.py` are hand-written regexes, which is the keyword-list failure ARCH §2.4 names. Should they be embedding centroids like the router?

THE ANSWER — YES FOR RECALL, NO FOR THE LABEL, and the split is what makes loose recall SAFE:
- Ground truth is the rest of the same commit. That puts the bank at the top of the provenance hierarchy (Constructed > Mechanical > StrongModelJudged). An embedding deciding what COUNTS as an episode would demote it to judged — and judged by a model of the same family we then evaluate, which is circular.
- Precision is recovered DOWNSTREAM by a mechanical test that needs no model: does the exemplars' expanded rule, applied to the truth's old text, reproduce the truth (tier A), or does the truth introduce/remove tokens the exemplars did (tier B), or nothing at all (tier C)?
- MEASURED COST OF LOOSE RECALL: 663/711 = 93% mechanically predictable, so the false-positive ceiling is 7%. Scoring the strict A+B bank moves the headline under three points (missed-fire 60.2% -> 58.4%, wrong-fire 21.2% -> 19.2%).

THE RECALL GAP IS REAL AND MEASURED: 6 of 12 detectors are pure diff shape (language-agnostic), 6 depend on a hand-written regex/idiom table. ALL FOUR under-quota shapes are the table-dependent ones (guard_insert, enum_match_arm, doc_sync on regexes; error_conversion on a hardcoded idiom list). Per-language yield for table-dependent shapes: rust 57 / go 54 / java 56 vs c 1 / kotlin 5 / scala 6. A centroid degrades gracefully where a regex fails hard — a Swift `import` sits near a Rust `use` in embedding space and matches no pattern in IMPORT_RE.

NOT SCHEDULED. Embedding recall grows four shapes and the long-tail languages; it does NOT change the 58.4% missed-fire finding, already solid at n=663.

BUILDING THE AUDIT CORRECTED THE AUDIT TWICE — read the sample, do not trust the classifier:
- An earlier version reported 14% coincidence. Reading 6 tier-C cases showed FIVE WERE GENUINE.
- Bug 1: tier A tested the MINIMAL diff. `radii`->`radius` has minimal diff `i`->`us`, and `line.replace("i","us")` mangles every other `i`. Must use `expand_rule`, which absorbs the identifier run — the same thing the rule lane matches on.
- Bug 2: tier B SUBTRACTED the anchor token as "not independent evidence". When a developer adds a field named `file_permission_api_name` and adds it again elsewhere, that identifier IS the intent and is also the anchor.
- Bug 3: an over-large STOP list ate `None`, scoring `json_list(&state)` -> `json_list(&state, None)` as coincidence.
After fixes: 86% -> 93% predictable, and literal_fanout went to 100% tier A.
