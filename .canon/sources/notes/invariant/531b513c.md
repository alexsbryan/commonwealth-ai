# The P1 provenance strip runs over POST-quote-verification text, and the two honesty layers agree by construction — do not read grounded=0…

The P1 provenance strip runs over POST-quote-verification text, and the two honesty layers agree by construction — do not read grounded=0 as a segmentation failure.

Observed live on the desktop 2026-08-10 (test-artifacts/p1-desktop-on-grounded.json): a turn asked to copy one sentence word-for-word produced 29 segments, 0 grounded. Cause, traced:
1. The 2B fixture model mangled its own "verbatim" quote. Source (tests/e2e/real/fixtures/corpus/meridian-lighthouse.txt:5) reads "The tower is 47 meters tall from base to lantern gallery"; the model emitted "47 métres tall", "47 mètrest all from base to lantenns gallery", "47mètres tall from base to lantern galleries".
2. The incumbent quote guardrail (sovereign-core/src/quote_verification.rs:126) scans quoted spans, fails the substring check, and DEMOTES each to "[unverified excerpt: …]" before release.
3. The P1 span resolver then segments the RELEASED text and reports those same spans Unverified.

So the wrapper text a reader sees ("[unverified excerpt: …]", and "Grounded in the source:" from runtime/grounding/mod.rs:920) is the RUNTIME'S OWN OUTPUT, not model prose and not a prompt scaffold the model echoed — I initially misread it as the latter and retracted. The two layers are consistent, not redundant: a genuinely verbatim span is never rewritten, so it survives unwrapped and resolves Grounded; only an unverified span gets the wrapper, and it was already going to segment Unverified.

Consequence for anyone measuring citability on a small model: grounded-segment counts are bounded by how often the model COPIES rather than paraphrases (the A/B saw 9 grounded of 185 segments, ~5%). A desktop witness that wants to exercise the openable-badge path needs a model that quotes, not more runs — tests/e2e/real/native-grounding-p1.real.spec.ts:258 skips with a stated reason rather than passing on 0 === 0.
