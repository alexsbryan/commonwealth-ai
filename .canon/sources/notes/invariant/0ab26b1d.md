# IndexMeta.enrichment_requested (renamed from enrichment_enabled, 2026-08-07, commit b1a6330b) means "the recipe ASKED for enrichment", NOT…

IndexMeta.enrichment_requested (renamed from enrichment_enabled, 2026-08-07, commit b1a6330b) means "the recipe ASKED for enrichment", NOT "enrichment completed". It is stamped `true` at the ENTRY of ingest's 'enrichment: block (corpus-engine/src/engine/ingest.rs) via CorpusIndex::set_enrichment_requested, and `false` on the investigation/atlas early-outs. Move that stamp to the block's exit (or to a success path) and EnrichmentChecker goes dead again: its whole job is to answer "was it?" about corpora that asked, so a success-only stamp erases exactly the failure case. Keep #[serde(alias = "enrichment_enabled")] on both IndexMeta and IndexInfo — every _corpus_meta.json written before the rename spells it the old way. RegistryEntry.enrichment_enabled (corpus-engine/src/registry.rs) is a DIFFERENT fact (the catalogue recipe) and must stay named as it is; the rename exists to keep the two apart.
