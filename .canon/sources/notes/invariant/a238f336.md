# Anything injected into the retrieval pool AFTER the pipeline must carry its own reserve — late injection lands past cap_and_reserve…

Anything injected into the retrieval pool AFTER the pipeline must carry its own reserve — late injection lands past `cap_and_reserve` (retrieval_pipeline.rs step 13), where the pool's reserve passes live.

Measured 2026-08-10 (invariant 3035f3a4, order raptor-late-inject-placement). `SOVEREIGN_RAPTOR_LATE` (default on) injects RAPTOR summaries post-rerank at two sites — `retrieval/mod.rs` (deep) and `handlers/knowledge_query.rs` (KQ). Both APPENDED at the tail and justified it in comments with "the summaries fill remaining budget, which DeepQuery's larger budget admits". FALSE: on summary_proof_theory the deep prompt was pool=40, admitted=28, raptor_admitted=0 of 8, char_budget=16000. A tail-placed chunk sits behind every leaf, so `format_scored_chunks_counted`'s char budget cuts it first and the bench's pool truncate drops the same tail. Late-inject was not making RAPTOR QA-neutral on big-pool turns, it was silently disabling it.

The fix needed NO new mechanism: `reserve_raptor_chunks` (question_analysis.rs) already existed and is exactly why the EARLY path never had this bug. Late-injected summaries were the only RAPTOR chunks in the system with no reserve. Both sites now call it after `apply_raptor_grounding`. Order-only — chunk SET identical, post-rerank TIMING (what buys leaf-ranking neutrality) untouched.

Result under production defaults, no flag default changed: raptor_admitted 0 -> 8 on every deep turn; the loop's retrieval objective 0/7 -> 7/7 sep cases at/above the baseline column; source_ratio 1.000 before AND after (no 1-for-1 eviction — summaries are shorter than leaves, so the admitted COUNT rose 28 -> 29).

Two general lessons:
1. When you move an injector past a pipeline, audit which pipeline STEPS it just skipped. Timing and placement are separable; only timing was load-bearing here.
2. A design claim about budget admission ("the larger budget admits them") is unverifiable without a per-turn admission event. The `prompt_admission` glassbox event (retrieval/mod.rs, target retrieval_audit) is what turned a 2-month-old plausible comment into a measured falsehood. Reach for RUST_LOG=retrieval_audit=info before trusting any statement about what the prompt carried.
