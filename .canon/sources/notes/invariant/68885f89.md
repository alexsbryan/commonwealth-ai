ConvDoc::turns is the PARSED SUBSET of a conversation, NOT its shape — read ConvDoc::chunk_ids for shape (Wrapped meshapp, 2026-07-26).

A chunk yields Turn rows only where its text carries a "### [ts] role" header. On conversations-anthropic, 13,373 of 16,404 chunks (82%) carry none: they are mid-answer CONTINUATION FRAGMENTS that begin mid-sentence (sampled: "aracter, though downtown's bones are good..."), i.e. the chunker split a long assistant answer.

Consequence when violated: fold_turn derived its chunk sequence from d.turns, so conversations fell below TURN_MIN_CHUNKS=8 and were skipped wholesale. It saw 1,510 of 15,283 seams across 135 of 425 eligible conversations — 10% of the archive. Fixed by adding ConvDoc::chunk_ids (every chunk id, ascending, parsed or not) in build_conv_docs and reading geometry from it: now 15,283/15,283 seams, 425/425 conversations, 388 qualified pivots.

The corollary is a LICENCE, not just a warning: parse-failure <=> the chunk holds no turn boundary, so an unparsed chunk cannot hide a user turn. Therefore "the last user turn before the seam" is definitionally correct at ANY chunk distance, and the quote walk must NOT be bounded by chunk distance. Measured over 388 qualified seams: the back-side turn is 3 chunks away at the mode and 0 chunks away only 0.5% of the time; the forward side is 0 chunks 93.8% of the time. A 3-chunk window discarded a correct quote on ~70% of them (both-sided coverage 62% -> 30%).
