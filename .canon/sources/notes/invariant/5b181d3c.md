A TRANSFORMERS UPGRADE IN THE TRAINING VENV SILENTLY BROKE GGUF CONVERSION — AND THE FAILURE ONLY SURFACES AFTER HOURS OF TRAINING. Hit 2026-08-02 on the M2 Max, verifier-v0 mix study.

SYMPTOM. `convert_hf_to_gguf.py` dies with `NotImplementedError: BPE pre-tokenizer was not recognized - update get_vocab_base_pre()`. Nothing about the model, the fuse, or the tokenizer files is wrong.

CAUSE. `get_vocab_base_pre` identifies a tokenizer by sha256-hashing the TOKEN IDS of a fixed check string, then matching that against a table of known hashes. The hash therefore depends on the installed `transformers`, not just on the tokenizer files. Measured against a byte-identical `tokenizer.json` (sha 5f9e4d49…):

| venv | transformers | chkhsh | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| `.venv` (training + eval) | 5.14.1 | 1444df51… | MISMATCH -> raises |
| `.venv-bespoke` | 4.49.0 | d30d75d9… | matches the `qwen35` entry |

PROOF IT IS THE ENV, NOT THE ARTIFACT: the known-good Jul 29 fused dir (`runs/probe-0.8b-orpo/fused-manual`, whose GGUF converted fine at the time) ALSO fails today under `.venv`. Same bytes, different answer.

FIX (no install, no downgrade of the training venv): run the converter under `.venv-bespoke/bin/python` with `PYTHONPATH=$HOME/dev/llama.cpp/gguf-py`. llama.cpp ships its own `gguf` package there, so it is version-matched to the converter by construction. `.venv-bespoke` has no pip (uv venv) — do not try `pip install gguf`.

WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND THE ONE ERROR. The GGUF step runs AFTER training, so a broken converter costs the entire training run before it announces itself. `scripts/run_mix_study.sh` now recomputes the chkhsh in preflight and refuses to start unless it appears in the converter, i.e. the GGUF leg is proven before an arm trains. Any pipeline of the shape train -> convert -> serve -> score needs that ordering inverted in its preflight.
