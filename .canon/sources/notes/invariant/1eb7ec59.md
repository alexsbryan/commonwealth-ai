STREAM B (i2_adversarial) VOLUME IS CAPPED BY THE GROUNDED SIDE, NOT BY THE CORRUPTION TAXONOMY OR BY `--n` (2026-07-31, measured on the Halo).

M2_STREAM_B.md's volume plan says the side tables "raise cases/claim toward 10" and that volume comes from more substrate. The first half is wrong and the second is right for a reason the doc doesn't state. Three mechanisms, all in adversarial.rs:

1. `make_case` ids every case `{kind}:{item.id}` (line 998), so each (kind, item) pair yields AT MOST ONE case — `seen` dedups on that id. Hard ceiling = items x 10.
2. `generate_cases` picks the wanted label from `out.len() % 2` (line 455) — strict U/G alternation. So total <= 2 x min(achievable_ungrounded, achievable_grounded) + 1.
3. Only 3 of the 10 kinds are grounded, and `verbatim` (914) and `reframe` (934) share ONE gate: `salient_terms(&claim, 3)` must be non-empty and ALL terms `value_present` in the evidence window. They therefore ALWAYS return identical counts — a fast tell that this is the binding path.

MEASURED, chaos-saltgrass, 103 harvested claims, seed 17, `--n 5000`:
  grounded = verbatim 42 + reframe 42 + multi_hop 19 = 103  (33% of the 3x103 ceiling)
  ungrounded = 104 (alternation-matched, NOT its own ceiling of 7x103=721)
  total = 207 = 2.0 cases/claim
Raising `--n` from 1000 to 5000 changed nothing. Only 41% of extracted claims carry 3 salient terms LITERALLY present in their window — `extract_claim_list` paraphrases, and the fairness contract correctly refuses to call a paraphrase a positive.

CONSEQUENCE FOR PLANNING: cases ~= 2 x claims, and pairs ~= 0.75 x cases after teacher discards. Both chaos corpora together (~1,300 claims) therefore land near ~2.6k cases / ~2k pairs — an order of magnitude under VERIFIER_V0.md §3's 20-40k target. Plan Stream B volume off claim count, never off `--n`, and do not expect side tables to move the total: entities/distractors feed ONLY ungrounded kinds, which were never the constraint. What they do buy is taxonomy COVERAGE (entity_swap and distractor_absorption go from 0 to represented), which is what the mix study and the §10 span axis need.

IF VOLUME MUST RISE, the levers are, in order: (a) more substrate claims; (b) relax the grounded gate from 3 salient terms to 2 — but that trades away exactly the fairness the contract exists to protect, so it needs its own measurement, not a flag flip; (c) more grounded KINDS (unit conversion, within-window multi-hop variants) — additive, since each new grounded kind adds up to `items` more cases and lifts the alternation cap with it.
