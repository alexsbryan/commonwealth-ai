support=1 IN THE NEXT-EDIT RULE LANE IS TWO DIFFERENT POPULATIONS, AND THE THRESHOLD IS LOAD-BEARING FOR ONLY ONE OF THEM (measured 2026-08-06, golden set at d75a9c4c).

`induce` (next_edit.rs:141) counts how many INDUCIBLE units in the 8-unit window carry a rule EQUAL to the most recent one. So support=1 never means "the user made one edit" — it means one of two structurally opposite things:

  LONE      exactly one inducible unit in the window. 62 positives, 2 negatives (neg/pos 0.03).
  DISAGREE  two or more inducible units whose rules differ. 55 positives, 87 negatives (neg/pos 1.58).

The golden negative bank is BUILT out of the second: neg_divergent (63), neg_literal_trap (10), neg_dissimilar (8), neg_revert. That is why the support>=2 threshold works — it is catching contradiction, and "support" is a proxy for it.

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR ANY FUTURE THRESHOLD WORK: measuring the two halves together hides the signal. An ROC over rule-find LENGTH across the combined population shows no separation at any cutoff, which reads as "support=1 is simply unsafe". Split them and the separation is 50x. Firing only on DISAGREE was measured as a control and is catastrophic exactly as predicted: 87 negatives fire, wrong-fire 15.3% -> 34.7%.

This did NOT ship. Firing on LONE alone still tops out at +4 useful / +1 wrong across a 20-cell grid, which does not pay for re-introducing the support tier 833ea5c1 deleted. It is recorded because the STRUCTURE is durable and re-deriving it costs a session: expand_rule returns None for no-ops and MULTI-LINE edits, so a multi-line insertion payload (field_init) yields no inducible unit at all and lands in `no_rule`, while a mid-burst keystroke artifact yields a junk one that becomes the whole of support=1.
