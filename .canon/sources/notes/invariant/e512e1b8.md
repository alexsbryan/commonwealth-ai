UNSCOPED RETRIEVAL ON A DEV HOST MEASURES THE DEV HOST, NOT THE PRODUCT (2026-08-26). Found while building the R1 coverage instrument for aa-omniscience; it applies to ANY grounded bench or eval run on a workstation, not just this lane.

=== THE MECHANISM ===
`chat inspect` / the grounded answer path fan out over EVERY installed corpus. This machine has ~40, and most are dev fixtures and code indexes: sep-*, enron-sample-*, chaos-*, corp-sheets-*, commonwealth-ai-self-atlas, folder-fixture-*. A deployment would have wikipedia and maybe two others.

Measured, n=6 smoke: for AA item qid=301 — a Law question about the D.C. Circuit's 1992 mandamus decision on OSHA's cadmium rulemaking — the global top-10 chunks by score included `score_wrong_specialization_returns_none` and `requirements_default_is_local_only`, i.e. RUST UNIT TESTS FROM THIS REPOSITORY. Same for qid=501 (a C23 union-attribute question): four of the top chunks were oicp-types test bodies.

So an `r` (retrieval coverage) or any grounded score measured unscoped on this host is priced against a corpus set no user has. Scope to the corpus under test (`--scope wikipedia` in coverage.py) and report the unscoped number beside it as a declared substitution (§18.3), never instead of it.

=== SECOND FINDING: A GOLD-STRING GREP IS NOT A COVERAGE INSTRUMENT ===
Both `gold_string_present` hits in that smoke were noise — gold "6" matching a digit inside Rust source, gold ";" matching semicolons. A short or single-character gold answer matches essentially any passage. And in the other direction, on the Mentuhotep/Intef item "Herakleopolis" appears 4x in the top-10 as the Tenth Dynasty's capital while the fact asked for (the city assigned to Intef son of Tjefi) is absent entirely. Over-counts on topical adjacency, over-counts on short strings, under-counts on paraphrase. Coverage needs an entailment judge. This is written into PREREG.md and into COVERAGE_GRADER_TEMPLATE example 3 so it cannot be re-derived by accident.

=== THIRD FINDING: A ONE-SIDED CONTROL CANNOT VALIDATE A JUDGE ===
The registered validation was a shuffled-context negative control (judge item i's question against item j's context, require NOT COVERED). It catches a judge answering from its own weights. It does NOT catch a judge that always answers B — that judge passes at 100% and reports r=0.0, which is indistinguishable from a corpus that holds nothing. The n=6 smoke returned exactly that shape and the summary alone could not separate the two. FIX: a positive control that PLANTS the fact mid-passage (not at the front, so recency cannot carry it) in the item's own retrieved context and requires COVERED, bar >=0.90. Both controls now gate the report: `summarize` emits no r unless both pass. Measured after the fix: shuffled 3/3 NOT_COVERED, positive 6/6 COVERED — the judge discriminates in both directions, so a zero from it is readable.

GENERAL RULE, worth applying to every judge we build: a negative control and a positive control are not optional halves. One alone leaves a constant-output judge indistinguishable from a real signal, in whichever direction the control does not test.

=== ALSO CORRECTED ===
The 2026-08-25 reading "gold-answer hits in retrieved chunks: 0", which drove the pessimistic read on retrieval, was an INSTRUMENT ARTIFACT: `chat inspect` defaults to `--format text --snippet 200` and truncated the chunk before the match. Identical command at `--snippet 1200` returns 2. Retrieval was not what failed there.
