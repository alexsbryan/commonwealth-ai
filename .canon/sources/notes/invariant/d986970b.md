THE THREE REQUEST-SIDE RESHAPE PASSES ARE MUTUALLY EXCLUSIVE, AND ONE OF THEM IS UNREACHABLE ON THE COMMITTED CORPUS.

Measured 2026-08-31 while building commonwealth/crates/commonwealth-api/tests/main/turn_reshape_fidelity.rs.

apply_failure_nudge_chat, apply_anti_repetition_chat and apply_read_attractor_nudge_chat (routes_inference.rs:245, inside `if turn_fidelity::reshape_enabled()`) all begin with has_recent_runtime_nudge(messages) (frontdoor.rs:1027), so EXACTLY ONE fires per turn. Consequence: on gym fixture 004 — the only committed turn whose tail repeats — failure-recovery claims the turn first and anti-repetition returns early every time. Deleting apply_anti_repetition_chat outright left every predicate over the 9 gym fixtures GREEN. The pack therefore carries an in-test witness turn (chat_with_a_repeated_successful_command: 3 identical exec_command calls, all exit 0) rather than a synthetic gym fixture, because gym/fixtures is the Codex gym's replay corpus and adding turns changes what the gym measures.

SECOND FACT, load-bearing for anyone reading turn_fidelity.rs: the nudges do far more than "append a synthetic message" (which is what the doc said until this commit). apply_read_attractor_nudge_chat DELETES every read-classified tool_call/result pair, DROPS the frontdoor's own compressed-history message, and REPLACES the caller's system prompt with a write-mandate. Measured: fixture 007 arrives with 12 messages and reaches the model with 4.

The differential predicate that pins this is scoped to messages + url_allowlist + evidence_id_allowlist, NOT the whole request: the handler legitimately rewrites request.model (slot targeting at routes_inference.rs ~168, ATOS pipeline ~203), so including it would make a fidelity test into a model-routing test.
