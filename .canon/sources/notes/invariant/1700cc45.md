THE ORDER UNDERSTATED THE SCOPE: this is paid ONCE PER LANE, not once per run. `bench all` subprocesses `svrn eval run`, which calls the same `build_session`. So routing, retrieval-prod (TWICE — that lane runs two commands), synth and chaos-monkey each paid ~23 s; `enrichment-f1` is in-process with no session, which is exactly why its measured cost is 0 s. Roughly 115 s of the 1,800 s budget.

THE LOAD IS NOT THE DAEMON'S — the order's stop condition did not fire. It is `sovereign-runtime-recipe::build_lane`, reached by all four hosts. Desktop, server and cli-daemon declare `LaneWarmth::Deferred`; the CLI one-shot declared `Eager` and was the only one. Fixed with a new host input `LaneScope` (see note 63ba739b); the three services keep `All`, byte-identical. After: lane startup (daemon-probe to slot-resolved) 23.11 s -> 4.49 s, -80.6%.

THE COST IS THE MODEL'S. Draft is ONE call per question producing ~1,170 completion tokens — 124.5 s for 1,022 tokens on q1 is ~8.2 tok/s on the 35B at load 16, against 50.7 tok/s quiet. Audit is the grounding gate's own 10-11-call ladder. No startup, no corpus load, no retry bug. So the answer is N, not a patch: subset cut 5 -> 3 (`synth-v2`), the three cheapest spanning three distinct archetypes, runway derived as 1,800 - 698 (other six lanes) - 409 (chaos after the seal) = 693 s at a 210 s median.

TWO HAZARDS FOR ANYONE READING SYNTH TIMINGS:

1. The two questions whose grounding judge FAILED OPEN (`judge_failure.calls_attempted 1, calls_answered 0`) had audits of 213 ms and 6 ms; the three whose judge answered ran 10-11 calls at 61.7-193.1 s. A contended host makes the judge fail open, which makes synth look FASTER while verifying LESS. Never read this lane's wall clock without the judge-failure counters.
2. `bench all --synth` emits NO per-question progress. The instrumented run sat silent for 525 s on its first question; a lane killed at its cap yields nothing at all, which is why the ledger had to come from `chat ask` rather than from the lane itself.

BASELINE CONSEQUENCE, stated because it is easy to miss: cutting synth-v1 to synth-v2 moves `smoke_selection_digest` and therefore the stack fingerprint, so BOTH baselines minted for `d1255ea4d79f` (chat-ask, throughput) stop being comparable. The next run reports "0 of 8 lanes have a comparable baseline for this stack" and must re-mint. That is the digest working as smoke.toml's own comment says it will, not a regression.
