# Desktop persistence tests: 'navigate away and back' does NOT prove persistence in this app. App.svelte keeps surfaces mounted and hides…

Desktop persistence tests: 'navigate away and back' does NOT prove persistence in this app. App.svelte keeps surfaces mounted and hides them with display:none (innerWorkMounted, .inner-work-layer), so component state survives navigation whether or not anything was written to storage. inner-work-page.spec.ts navigated away and back, asserted the draft returned, and PASSED under a mutation that made every saveDraft() a localStorage.removeItem() — hole-inner-work-draft-persistence. A real persistence assertion needs either a fresh page load (bootToChat again — localStorage survives the goto) or a direct page.evaluate read of the storage key. Both are in specs/inner-work-draft-persistence.spec.ts. Also: innerWorkSession keys use LOCAL date parts, not toISOString() — a UTC-keyed spec reads the wrong slot after 00:00Z.
