FLASH-NEXT'S LATENCY IS COLD-START PREFILL, NOT SLOW GENERATION — REPUBLISHED 2026-08-27 WITH THREE OF ITS OWN CLAIMS CORRECTED. Supersedes 4993e07d. The central finding held up under measurement; three supporting details did not, and one of them was a phantom bug report.

=== WHAT STILL STANDS (the load-bearing part) ===
Generation is fine and it is not thinking: 44 primary calls, MEDIAN OUTPUT 35 CHARACTERS, ~22.4 tok/s standalone. The wall time is prefill, and most of it is COLD prefill. Of a 5-question run's 1384.5s: primary inference 613.7s (27 cold prefills = 566s; 16 pinned hits = 39s, avg 2.5s), fast slot 85.5s, and ~685s (~49%) NOT model inference at all. Per question the cold-start tax is ~101s = 82% of primary time. `prefix_cache_gate`'s veto of partial-KV reuse is CORRECT here and must not be chased: GGUF header says block_count=48, full_attention_interval=4 → 12 attention + 36 SSM layers, so partial keep would restore 12 layers' KV and leave 36 recurrent states stale.

=== CORRECTION 1 — THE GATHER COUNT WAS HALF THE REAL FIGURE ===
The original said "ngram_size=3, heads_per_ngram=8 → a 512-token ubatch does ~4,096 gathers". Wrong by 2×. qwen4exp.cpp:55 computes `ple_n_heads = (ple_ngram_size - 1) * ple_heads_per_ngram` = (3-1)×8 = 16 heads — 8 bigram + 8 trigram, confirmed by the header's 16-element `ple.head_offsets` / `ple.head_vocab_sizes`. So it is 16 rows/token (1,440 B at IQ4_NL's 90 B/row), 8,192 gathers per 512-token ubatch. This also reconciles the apparent 8-vs-16 disagreement with `research/engram/RESULTS.md`: both were right about different quantities. The CONCLUSION is unchanged and in fact strengthened — 1,440 B/token is still far too small to explain seconds, and `ple.layers = [1]` means ONE gather per forward pass, not one per layer.

=== CORRECTION 2 — THE env-gate BUG REPORT WAS A PHANTOM ===
The original ended: "SOVEREIGN_N_UBATCH … is NOT declared in quality/env-flags.toml — `cargo xtask env-gate` should be catching that." It is in `quality/baselines/env_unregistered.txt`, i.e. explicitly accepted as legacy debt, which is exactly why the gate stays quiet. The gate is behaving correctly. DO NOT go looking for a defect here. (Related gotcha found the same day: `cargo xtask` only resolves from `corpus-engine/` — the alias lives in that directory's `.cargo/config.toml`.)

=== CORRECTION 3 — THE bit-faithful CITATION WAS FOR THE WRONG ARCHES, AND HAS NOW BEEN RUN ON THE RIGHT ONE ===
The original justified the pinned-prefix cache with "restore is bit-faithful (32/32 greedy-identical on qwen35/qwen35moe hybrids)". NEITHER OF THOSE HAS PLE. Measured on qwen4exp 2026-08-27: 32/32 greedy-identical, restore 28 ms against a 5,302 ms prefill (189×). So the conclusion survives with a better citation — but see note `b588ba8b` for what it does and does not prove: `ple_hist` is genuinely not serialized and its reset genuinely fires on every restore; it simply lands ~18 positions upstream of the sampled token.

=== THE PREFILL DEFICIT: HYPOTHESIS KILLED, CAUSE NARROWED ===
The original proposed that 512 experts / 10 active makes each expert's prefill matmul tall-skinny and overhead-bound, testable via n_ubatch. TESTED, NO-GO: 1.16× across an 8× ubatch range against a pre-registered 2.0× bar, GTT cost only ~0.7 GiB (so memory was never the obstacle). A follow-up prompt-length sweep at fixed ubatch came back LINEAR — pp(16384) = 0.85 × pp(1024), no knee at 2048 — killing the sparse indexer (top_k=2048) as the driver too. Prefill cost is a PER-TOKEN CONSTANT. The surviving candidate is the DeltaNet recurrence: 36 of 48 layers, sequential in the token dimension, which gains nothing from a wider ubatch and costs linearly in n. That fits both sweeps. It is upstream-shaped and not fixable from this repo — which is why the pin-thrash fix was the tractable win. Bars and data: `research/engram/PRE-REGISTRATION-ubatch.md`.
