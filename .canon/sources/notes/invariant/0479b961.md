MEASURED 2026-08-19 — the exact conditions under which a multi-branch `oneOf` compiles under llguidance 1.7.6. I asserted this wrong TWICE from the incident note before testing; do not re-derive it from prose.

A multi-branch `oneOf` compiles ONLY when BOTH hold:
  (a) EVERY branch declares `"type": "object"`, AND
  (b) the branches are provably disjoint, by EITHER
      - a `const` discriminator on a shared required key, OR
      - `additionalProperties: false` with disjoint required-key sets.

Drop (a) and it fails even with discriminators on every branch. Drop (b) and it fails even with `type` on every branch. Failure message: "oneOf constraints are not supported. Enable 'coerce_one_of'…" — both `coerce_one_of` and `lenient` are false and never set in this repo.

WRONG THEORIES I HELD, each falsified by the test going red:
  1. "a branch losing `type: object` is the trigger" — false alone; with `const` present AND `type` absent it still fails, but the cause is (a), and a branch missing `type` while its twin has one still compiled in my first (badly constructed) case.
  2. "the `const` discriminator alone carries the proof" — false; bare branches with discriminators do NOT compile.

WHY IT MATTERS: both production `oneOf` schemas satisfy (a)+(b) today — the tool envelope (`inference_adapter.rs:920`, `name: {enum:[…]}`) and `markers.verdict_schema()` (`verdict: {const: …}`). An ordinary edit removing EITHER the per-branch `type` or the discriminator disarms the whole constraint. Before 2026-08-19 that was SILENT: `sampler.rs` warned and fell back to free-form sampling, returning 200 with unconstrained prose (the 2026-08-10 incident recorded in `markers.py:76-80`). It now REFUSES — see the paired decision note.

Also measured: llguidance hard-errors on unimplemented keywords rather than ignoring them — `uniqueItems`, `not`, `if`/`then`, `contains` all fail to compile. `minLength`, `minItems`, `pattern`, `enum`, `additionalProperties` ARE genuinely masked at logit level (not theater).

Pinned by three tests in `sovereign-inference/tests/llguidance_parity.rs`, each with a compiling twin so none can pass vacuously: `unimplemented_keyword_does_not_compile`, `multi_branch_oneof_needs_a_discriminator_to_compile`, `additional_properties_walker_does_not_rescue_a_broken_oneof`.
