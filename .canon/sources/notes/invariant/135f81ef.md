THE MESH'S INTERNAL PORT :9742 IS UNAUTHENTICATED AND BINDS 0.0.0.0 BY DEFAULT. Verified 2026-08-04 while scoping the internally-hosted-mesh (enterprise private cloud) plan.

THE FACTS:
- The mesh's ENTIRE auth surface is one equality check — incoming `Mesh` must carry the same `mesh_id` and `join_key_hash` — and it exists on the GOSSIP ROUTE ONLY (commonwealth-api/src/routes_internal/gossip.rs:28-45; the doc comment names itself "the auth boundary", 401 at :43).
- Every OTHER /internal/* route on :9742 has NO auth gate: gossip, join, model load/unload, RAW GGUF BYTES, corpus mutation, atlas state, and a fleet-wide pipeline pause with one-hop fanout.
- `internal_bind` defaults to "0.0.0.0" (sovereign-contracts/src/setup_config.rs:1043-1046). The CLIENT port :9741 defaults to 127.0.0.1 (:975) — do not conflate them.
- Consequence: anything that can route to :9742 on the corporate LAN can pull your model weights and mutate your corpora.

THIS IS A DOCUMENTED, DELIBERATE POSTURE, not a bug — sovereign/docs/ENTERPRISE_FLEET_DEPLOY.md:90-95 states it plainly ("trust the network boundary, not a per-request credential"). It is defensible for a household LAN. It is the reason enterprise IT says no.
