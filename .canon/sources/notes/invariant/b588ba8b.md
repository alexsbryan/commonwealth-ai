THE SOURCE FACTS IN d63781e3 ARE STILL TRUE — ONLY THE CONSEQUENCE WAS WRONG. `ple_hist` really is `mutable` on the MODEL (models.h:2288), really is absent from every state_read/state_write path, and the contiguity guard at qwen4exp.cpp:948 really does fire here: ctx A leaves `next_pos` at 1500+|suffix|+32 after generating, ctx B re-enters at 1500, they differ, and the history is reset to EOS padding. The two contexts therefore hashed suffix tokens 0 and 1 against DIFFERENT n-gram context, and produced identical greedy output anyway.

WHY IT DOES NOT SHOW, AND THIS IS THE PART THAT GENERALISES. The engram enters at ONE layer of 48 (`ple.layers = [1]`) through a sigmoid-gated residual, and the mis-hashed positions are at the START of the suffix while the sampled logits come from its LAST token — ~18 positions downstream. The perturbation has to survive 47 further layers and 18 positions of attention/DeltaNet mixing and then flip an argmax. It does not.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH — do not upgrade this to "restore is bit-faithful on qwen4exp". What is proven is narrower: greedy output over 32 tokens is unchanged for a 1500-token prefix and a ~20-token suffix. Not proven: that the effect is zero, or that it stays invisible when the sampling point sits INSIDE the 2-token reset window. The maximum-sensitivity probe is a 1-TOKEN suffix, where index 0 is both mis-hashed and the position sampled from; that has not been run. The production shape — restore a pin, then decode a long evidence+question tail — is the shape that WAS run, which is why this is actionable as-is.

CONSEQUENCE FOR THE PIN WORK. The pin-thrash fix (6102a3578) increases restore frequency, and this run says that is a clean win rather than a correctness trade.
