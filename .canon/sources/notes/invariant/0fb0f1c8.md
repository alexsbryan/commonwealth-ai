WHY IT MATTERS ANYWAY: the noun-convergence / duplicate-concept instrument asks "does this concept already exist HERE". A prior-art hit against llama.cpp's `LlamaFileType` or a swebench fixture is a false positive, and per concept_gate.rs a false-positive machine gets switched off in a week. The tail matters more than its share for this consumer.

FIX ORDER: constrain the prior-art lookup to git-tracked paths at QUERY time (cheap, structural, no re-ingest). An ingest-side scope filter is the right longer-term fix and is separate work — it costs a full re-index, 2h20m on this host.
