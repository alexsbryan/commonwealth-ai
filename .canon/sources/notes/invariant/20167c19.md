THE MEMORY GUARD IS FIXED AND THE PRO 5000 STILL CANNOT FINISH — TWO SEPARATE FACTS, MEASURED 2026-08-05 ON ONE RUN. Vast 46888730, RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell 47.27GB, $0.6681/hr, same recipe (ARM A, micro 1 x accum 32, seq 4096).

WHAT THE FIX BOUGHT: 15 CLEAN STEPS WHERE 3 RAN BEFORE, zero WARN, zero ABORT.
  tripwire ARMED: pool limit 43.5GB (92% of 47.27); unattributed 0.3GB; ceiling 43.2GB
  step  1  ours=35.89/42.9GB  reserved=44.26GB   <- load+optimizer-init transient
  step  3  34.77s/it  ours=29.86  reserved=44.36
  step 10  41.27s/it  ours=30.66  reserved=44.36
  step 15  46.62s/it  ours=29.88  reserved=44.36  gtt(devwide)=44.97
RESERVED SETTLED AT 44.36GB — THE EXACT FIGURE THAT ABORTED THIS CARD AT STEP 4 BEFORE. Identical allocator behaviour, opposite verdict. The false positive is dead, proven on the hardware that produced it. Adapter gate PASSED (max|B| 1.09e-03, 248/248 nonzero).

THEN A GENUINE OOM AT STEP 16 — NOT A GUARD TRIP:
  Tried to allocate 7.50 GiB; 2.41 GiB free of 47.27 GiB
  allocated by PyTorch  29.03 GiB   <- real demand, well under the ceiling
  reserved UNALLOCATED  15.23 GiB   <- FRAGMENTATION
PyTorch released what cache it could and still could not serve one 7.5 GiB contiguous request.

THREE THINGS THIS SETTLES.
1. STEADY DEMAND IS ~30GB, NOT 35.9. The per-step windowed peak (new gpu_step_peak_gb / mem_ours_gb columns) separates the step-1 load+optimizer-init transient (35.89) from what a training step needs (29.86-30.66). Every prior sizing used the 35.9 transient.
2. THE DEMAND-BASED GUARD CANNOT SEE FRAGMENTATION, BY CONSTRUCTION. It watches `allocated`; fragmentation lives in the gap between `reserved` and `allocated`. This is a known, accepted blind spot of the fix, not a defect to patch by re-adding a device-wide trip — that trip fires on healthy caching 12 steps before the real event.
3. THE OLD GUARD WAS ACCIDENTALLY RIGHT ON THIS CARD. It aborted at step 4 for a wrong reason (its own cache) on a card that does in fact fail at step 16. That coincidence is exactly why "just raise the 92%" was the wrong fix: it would have produced this same OOM with no explanation and no attribution.

WITHDRAWN: 201dbc4d's "the cheap tier is probably viable, the blocker is the guard". The guard was A blocker and is fixed. The card has a SECOND, real blocker.

NEXT LEVER, and it is the option that note ranked second: PYTORCH_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True, named in PyTorch's own error text. It makes reserve track demand and should remove the fragmentation. Being measured now on the same pod.
