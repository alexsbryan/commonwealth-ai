"DAEMON UNREACHABLE" DOES NOT MEAN THE DAEMON DIED — AND THE ERROR TEXT TELLS YOU TO DO THE WRONG THING (measured 2026-08-26, Alexs-MacBook-Pro-2). Extends e512e1b8.

=== WHAT HAPPENED ===
A 60-item retrieval sweep hit, on 45 consecutive items in ~90 seconds:
  `chat inspect exit 1: bootstrap failed: Serialization error: daemon unreachable
   at http://localhost:9741. Start it with `svrn daemon run`, or pass --daemon <url>`

The daemon had NOT died. PID 16031 was up 2h42m — it predated the sweep by hours — and `/v1/models` answered 200 immediately before and immediately after the window. Host conditions: 11% free memory, heavy pageouts, 8 concurrent cargo/rustc from a peer session's build, daemon RSS 39 GB. No jetsam/memorystatus entries in `log show`. The endpoint simply stopped accepting connections for roughly 30 seconds under that pressure, then resumed.

=== WHY THE MESSAGE IS DANGEROUS ===
It names exactly one remedy — "Start it with `svrn daemon run`" — and that remedy is WRONG here. Acting on it starts a SECOND daemon against a live one, on a host already out of memory. The message has no way to distinguish "not running" from "running and refusing", and it asserts the first. Anyone (agent or human) who trusts it during host pressure makes the situation worse. Treat this string as "could not connect", never as "not running": check `pgrep -fl sovereign-cli-daemon` and re-probe `/v1/models` before concluding anything.

=== THE HARNESS DEFECT IT EXPOSED, AND THE SHAPE OF THE FIX ===
`coverage.py` caught the exception per item and continued, with no retry. So a ~30s blip did not cost 30 seconds — it cost 45 of 60 items, permanently marked failed, in 90 seconds, because each failure returned instantly and the loop ran at full speed straight through the rest of the bank. The run "completed" its phase having measured a quarter of it.

TWO mechanisms, and any long harness against this daemon wants BOTH:
1. RETRY with backoff (5s/15s/45s) inside the call. A transient refusal is not an answer about the thing being measured.
2. A CIRCUIT BREAKER on consecutive failures (default 3). Retry alone is not enough — without a breaker a genuinely down endpoint still grinds the whole bank, just more slowly. Stop the phase, keep what is on disk, resume when the host is quiet.

What saved the RESULT (as opposed to the run) was the pre-existing coverage-1.0 gate: 15/60 would have summarized as COULD_NOT_JUDGE, not as a low r. Absence reported, never defaulted (§18.3). The gate is why a burned run produced no number instead of a wrong one — but the burn was still avoidable and is now fixed.

=== COORDINATION ===
This is invariant 849417cd ("no cargo gates while a flight is in progress") from the other side: the flight started INTO an existing build. On a shared workstation the flight cannot assume the host is its own, so the flight must be resilient rather than the build being polite.
