GOSSIP PEER SELECTION MUST BE STALENESS-ORDERED, NEVER RANDOM (2026-07-29, sovereign-mesh/src/gossip.rs `select_round_peers`). Supersedes f541d59e, which blamed the dead members. It was wrong about the cause — see REFUTED below.

THE RULE. Selection misses alone must NEVER be able to carry a reachable peer past `offline_threshold`. `run_one_round` shuffled ALL members and truncated to `FANOUT`(2), so a live peer's per-round contact chance was `FANOUT/members`, while `DEFAULT_OFFLINE_THRESHOLD`(60s) is sized "roughly 6× the interval"(10s) on the UNSTATED assumption that a round CONTACTS the peer. Random sampling silently violated the assumption its own constant was chosen under.

FIX: order by `last_contact` ascending (longest-unseen first), online group before offline group; offline peers take leftover slots. A peer that misses a round is PROMOTED rather than re-entered into a fresh lottery, which converts the coin flip into a bound: with n online peers a peer waits at most ceil(n/FANOUT) rounds. RNG is gone from the round entirely. Ceiling is now computable — `max_online_peers_before_false_offline` = FANOUT × (threshold/interval) = 12 online peers at shipped constants, asserted in a test so changing any of the three constants surfaces it.

REFUTED — the dead members were NOT the cause, only the rate. f541d59e said pruning the corpses was the hygiene fix. Simulated the OLD selection over 5000 rounds, FANOUT=2, 6-round budget; worst contact gap in rounds: 1 online + 2 offline → 8; 3 online + 0 offline → 12; 6 online → 26; 6 online + 2 offline → 32; 12 online → 59. THREE HEALTHY PEERS AND ZERO CORPSES already blows the budget. Random sampling was never sound here.

REACHABILITY IS THE ONLY AXIS, and this is a design constraint, not an implementation detail. Alex, 2026-07-29: "Mesh members have reasons to exist on the mesh even if they can't contribute to this bigger model run. It will be inevitable that a subset of members will participate in this." A peer that is online but cannot hold a 122B shard is gossiped with every round like any other. Test `selection_reads_reachability_only_never_capability` exists to keep capability from ever becoming an input.

WHY IT MATTERS: gossip-Online membership IS the RPC-worker liveness signal (deliberately — daemon.rs `Reaffirm::Held`, because under decode load the /status probe starves while gossip survives). Each gossip flap therefore empties the RPC eligible-worker set. Live on Meshsonics 2026-07-29 BeefyMac flapped Offline 3× in 14 min (staleness 68/63/62s) with reach_ms 65–600 either side of every lapse; the third retired a HEALTHY distributed 122B eleven minutes into serving ("no eligible RPC workers"). 14s after SIGKILL: `gossip: reach ok reach_ms=548`.

DO NOT "fix" a recurrence by raising DEFAULT_OFFLINE_THRESHOLD or FANOUT first. That trades one arbitrary constant for another and leaves the mechanism intact. Check `max_online_peers_before_false_offline` against the actual online count — if the mesh has outgrown it, THEN raise FANOUT deliberately.
