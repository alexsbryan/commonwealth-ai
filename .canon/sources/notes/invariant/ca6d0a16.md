One change per iteration in agent-loop calibration. When the agent gets a bad score, four hypotheses are alive at once: agent was bad, spec was bad, charter was bad, rubric was bad. Changing charter + spec + model on iteration 2 means you cannot attribute the score delta. Change-type hierarchy cheap→expensive: prompt-level (AGENTS.md/charter wording) → spec-level → charter-level (invariants/phases) → loop-level (model/scorer/rubric). Most learning happens in tiers 1-2.
