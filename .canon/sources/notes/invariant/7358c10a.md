SEP substrate shape on this host (measured 2026-08-08, native-grounding Phase 1):

- The 1,770 `~/.sovereign/indexes/sep-<slug>/` directories are ATLASES ONLY. Not one has a `chunks.lance`; only 2 of 1,770 source .md files survive under `~/.sovereign/corpora/sep/articles/`. Reading a sep-* dir alone makes it look like the passages are gone.
- They are not gone: passages live in ONE `sep` corpus of 187,967 chunks keyed by `source_doc_id`, with exactly 1,770 distinct values (https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/<slug>/), one per atlas. `flywheel::passages::chunk_store_for` is the single name for that mapping.
- Across all 42 installed corpora that HAVE a chunks.lance: 146,596 chunks carry non-null metadata and only 39 carry `section_id`. So `CorpusIndex::resolve_sections_to_chunks` (corpus-engine/src/index/read.rs:137), the purpose-built resolver for atom evidence refs like `sec_0002`, returns EMPTY for every corpus this initiative mines. Do not reach for it here.
- Working resolution is VERBATIM ANCHOR CONTAINMENT: an atom's quotable_excerpt / passage_preview / anchor are cut out of the source, so search the document's real chunks for one containing them (ellipsis-split, ordered, >=4-word fragments). Resolves 57.8% of SEP claims; the other 42% are paraphrase, not quotation, and are DROPPED and counted — never attached to the nearest-looking chunk.
- Wikipedia cannot feed claim mining at all: 800 MB atlas, 1,773,106 atoms, every one an Entity, ZERO Claim atoms.
