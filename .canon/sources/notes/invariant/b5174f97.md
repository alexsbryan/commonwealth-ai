DECISION→OUTCOME JOIN: exactly ONE terminal outcome per routing decision, keyed by `decision_id`. (sovereign-mesh/decision_log.rs, landed 2026-07-26.)

WHY IT IS AN INVARIANT: SCHEDULER_QUALITY.md §5 defines Tier-1 calibration as decision-AGREEMENT between the simulator and real hardware. `SchedulerTrace::join_rate()` is the admissibility gate on a captured trace — a trace below 1.0 is not evidence. Break the one-outcome-per-decision rule and the join rate becomes meaningless in both directions (duplicates inflate, gaps deflate).

CONSEQUENCES ALREADY ENCODED — do not "simplify" these away:
- Failed intermediate cascade steps do NOT emit their own outcome. They accumulate into `RoutingOutcome.failovers[]` and bump `attempt_index` on the ONE terminal record. `attempt_index > 0` is the §5 waste metric.
- `SinglePeerSelection` carries `decision_id`/`oicp_request_id` OUTSIDE its `Option<pick>`. A selection that picked nobody still made a decision (`stay_local`) and still needs an outcome; folding the ids into the `Some` arm silently drops every stay-local record from the join.
- `ThroughputObservedStream::drop` early-returns for zero-token streams — but ONLY when no `OutcomeContext` is attached. A zero-token dispatch must still close its join or it reads as a decision that never completed.
- A cascade that exhausts every step emits `ServedBy::Failed`. "The mesh could not serve this" must be a measurable result, not a gap.
- Gated decisions (`not_offload_eligible`, `no_routing_signal`, `operator_disabled`, `envelope_absent`) DO emit records — they are the arrival stream the sim needs to reproduce local load. They log at `debug` (not `info`) so Fast-class housekeeping traffic doesn't flood the daemon log; the record itself is emitted either way.

Pinned by `tests/scheduler_decision_records.rs` (10 tests) — every routing surface asserts `outcome.decision_id == decision.decision_id`.
