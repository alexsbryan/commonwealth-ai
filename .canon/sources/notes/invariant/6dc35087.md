EVERY PROMPT-BUILDING SITE MUST GO THROUGH `synthesize::bounded_evidence` — and the bound must never drop by POSITION (2026-08-24, commit 14ddccf49).

Watched red in the field: the first genuinely broad acquisition (Tavily, 50 chunks, 1,360,782 chars, 40 distinct sources, under two minutes) killed the loop with `Prompt too long: 302,153 tokens meets or exceeds the context window of 65,532`.

WHY NO CAP CAUGHT IT: `evidence_window_max_chunks` is 100 and the run held 50. That cap counts CHUNKS. A web chunk is ~50x fatter than an estate one — measured median 26,766 chars vs 521, with `fetch::CHUNK_CONTENT_CAP` allowing 50,000. Acquisition breadth and prompt size were coupled with nothing in between. Three sites built an evidence block; only the composer's sections were bounded, and only incidentally (via `rank_passages`).

THE FILL ORDER IS THE LOAD-BEARING PART. A per-chunk share + truncate loses the TAIL of every long page silently, and a page's most specific material is as likely to sit at the bottom as the top — the artificial-cutoff info-loss this repo has been bitten by before. `bounded_evidence` works on PASSAGES (whole page stays reachable) and fills either ranked (embedder available) or round-robin across sources (no embedder). Position never selects.
Watch-it-fail: document-order fill => `rotation_gives_every_source_a_turn_before_any_source_repeats` fails with `source 1 missing` — a 4-passage budget hands all four to source 0 and 3 of 4 fetched sources never reach the model.
`passages_used + passages_dropped` always equals the total; drops are logged with source counts.

LATENT BUG IT EXPOSED (did not create): `figure_inventory` walked every chunk in full while instructing "every evidence-supported figure must appear in the answer". Against a bounded prompt that orders the model to produce numbers it cannot see — a demand to invent, aimed at what the audit checks hardest. Inventory now derives from `BoundedEvidence.admitted`, asserted by test. ANY future view derived from the evidence must derive from `admitted`, never from the window.
