Watcher shutdown senders must live INSIDE the spawned task's async block, not in the outer function scope (sovereign-mesh/src/daemon.rs, 2026-05-10).

Wrong pattern (WikipediaNewsworthyWatcher's original wiring): `let _hold = tx` bound in start_daemon's scope. When start_daemon returns, _hold drops, sender drops, watcher's shutdown_rx.changed() returns Err inside its tokio::select!, watcher returns. No ticks ever fire even though the daemon logs 'WikipediaNewsworthyWatcher started' + 'newsworthy.watcher_starting'.

Right pattern (storage-snapshot loop, same file ~50 lines above): wrap in `tokio::spawn(async move { let _hold_shutdown_tx = tx; let h = watcher.spawn(rx); let _ = h.await; })`. The sender is owned by the wrapping spawn-task and lives as long as it awaits the inner handle — i.e. for the daemon's lifetime.

Surfaced only after adding corpus_engine=info to the tracing filter (so newsworthy.watcher_starting became visible). The absence of any newsworthy.tick events in the 9 hours / 7 daemon restarts that preceded the discovery is what proved the watcher was dying immediately after logging start.

Applies to any future long-running watch::channel-shutdown watcher in sovereign-mesh/daemon.rs. Mirror the storage-snapshot wrapper, not the original newsworthy pattern.
