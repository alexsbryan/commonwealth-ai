M2_HALO_GRADCHECK.md:449 records the condition arm A actually ran under: "Arm A ran on an EMPTY BOX — daemon stopped, GTT at launch 620 MiB — with per-process attribution on." That is a protocol term, not an incidental detail, and it is not in launch_arm.sh.

WHY IT DECIDES THE RUN. GTT on this box is one 124 GB pool shared by the trainer and any resident model. Arm A's envelope reached 101.97 GB from a 0.6 GB baseline. The daemon with a 4B primary + 0.8B fast + embed holds ~8.5 GB. Same envelope on top of that baseline is ~110 GB against a 112 GB tripwire with a 3-consecutive-sample rule — so an arm that is behaving identically to arm A aborts somewhere around step 115, one or two steps short of the 117 it is trying to match, for a reason that has nothing to do with training. The failure would look like "AB is less stable than A" and it would be a co-tenant.

BEFORE ANY UNBUCKETED ARM: `sovereign daemon stop`, confirm `mem_info_gtt_used` is under ~1 GB, then launch. launch_arm.sh prints "box GTT at launch" — read it. Anything over ~1 GB means the run is not comparable to arm A and the tripwire margin is gone. Restore the daemon afterwards (config backup at ~/.svrnmesh/config.toml.bak-pre-armAB-20260803 holds the DeepSeek-V4-Flash primary arrangement).

NOTE THE CONVERSE: this is specific to UNBUCKETED runs reproducing arm A. With length bucketing on (the default since 2026-08-03) the envelope is much lower and a resident daemon is affordable — bucketing is what buys back the co-tenancy headroom.
