# Every push in the enrichment initiative (T1 remainder onward) must end with a demonstration of value mapped to user-facing wins for the…

Stated 2026-07-31 at T1-remainder funding: an essential part of the enrichment initiative going forward is that the end of every push must demonstrate the value of the investment to the product team, mapped to user-facing wins.

Why: The T1 tranche is measurement-fabric work (canaries, lanes, gates) whose value is easy to state in engineering terms and easy to miss in product terms. The operator needs each push to close with a product-legible artifact, not just green gates.

How to apply: Each T1 step (and future tranche steps) carries a "user-facing win" framing in its plan entry (~/.claude/plans/t1-remainder-trust.md §Demonstrating value). At the end of every push, produce the demo artifact: a before/after metric, a red-light-that-would-have-shipped example, or a concrete answer-quality comparison — in language about what the user experiences (trustworthy summaries, no silent quality decay, honest abstention), not lane internals.
