# Larkspur Commons — charter and decisions

A four-section probe corpus for the `governance` ontology template. Invented
house, invented decisions. Its job is the P4 equivalence case: two decisions
that state ONE rule in two surface forms — "must not host after 10pm" and
"must end hosting by 10pm" — must come out as one rule and NOT as a conflict.
See `hosting.truth.json`.

## Article VII — Hosting in the common room

Members may host guests in the common room. Hosting must end by 10pm on
weeknights so that residents on the ground floor can sleep. A member who
hosts is responsible for their guests until the guests have left the
building.

## Decision 2025-03-14 — hosting curfew restated

The March meeting reviewed Article VII after two complaints. The meeting
agreed that members must not host in the common room after 10pm on
weeknights. No change to the substance of Article VII was intended; the
wording was restated because "hosting must end by 10pm" was read by some
members as permitting arrivals before 10pm who then stayed later.

## Decision 2025-06-02 — quiet hours moved

The June meeting moved quiet hours in the common room from 10pm to 11pm on
Fridays and Saturdays. Weeknight quiet hours are unchanged at 10pm. Hosting
on Friday and Saturday nights may therefore continue until 11pm.

## Decision 2025-06-02 — guest parking

Guests may park in the visitor bay for up to four hours. The bay is not for
overnight guest parking; overnight guests park on the street.
