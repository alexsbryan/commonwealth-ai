 A920 
 The A920 is the revolutionary device that started it all. A stunning and ergonomically designed terminal, this SmartMobile delivers elegance, style, and security to every payment experience. This mobile POS combines the full features of an Android tablet with a powerful payment terminal, all in a sleek and compact design. This Android device features an integrated camera, high-speed thermal printer, and high-capacity battery, designed to meet the daily demands of all dynamic retail and hospitality environments.

 PAX A920 Data Sheet 
 First-Class Performance 
 The PAX A920 terminal is powered by an ARM Cortex-A7 processor, which offers a balance of performance and energy efficiency ideal for mobile payment terminals. This processor ensures smooth operation of the device’s Android-based system, enabling fast and reliable transaction processing.

 PAXBiz® Powered by Android™ 
 The device runs on PAXBiz®, a secure and customizable platform built on Android™, offering a familiar interface and access to a wide range of business apps. This combination enhances user experience while allowing merchants to tailor the device to their specific operational needs. 
 Ergonomic Design 
 The device features a sleek, curved body that fits comfortably in the hand, making it easy for merchants to carry and operate throughout the day. Its lightweight design and intuitive touchscreen layout enhance usability, reducing strain during prolonged use and improving overall efficiency at the point of sale. 
 Capture Every Detail 
 It’s equipped with a rear-facing camera that enables barcode and QR code scanning, streamlining inventory management and customer transactions. Its integrated imaging capabilities support a variety of business applications, enhancing the device’s versatility in retail and service environments. 
 Touchscreen Display 
 It features a vibrant 5-inch capacitive touchscreen that delivers a clear, high-resolution display for easy navigation and signature capture. Its responsive multi-touch interface enhances user interaction, making transactions faster and more intuitive for both merchants and customers. 
 Payment Acceptance Versatility 
 The A920 supports a wide range of payment methods, including EMV chip & PIN, magnetic stripe, and NFC contactless transactions such as Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Samsung Pay. It also enables QR code payments and mobile wallets, making it a flexible, user-friendly solution for modern retail environments that require seamless integration, enhanced customer convenience, and support for evolving digital payment trends. 
 Long-Lasting Battery 
 The A920 device is powered by a high-capacity 5250mAh lithium-ion battery, designed to support all-day operation without frequent recharging. This extended battery life ensures reliable performance in busy retail and service environments, even during peak hours. 
 Built-In Printer 
 The A920 includes a high-speed thermal printer that produces clear, professional receipts on the spot. Its compact, integrated design ensures seamless operation without adding bulk, making it ideal for mobile and countertop use. 
 Advanced Connectivity 
 The A920 supports dual-band Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz and 5 GHz), ensuring fast and stable wireless connections for seamless transaction processing. This reliable connectivity allows merchants to operate efficiently in various environments, from retail counters to mobile service locations. 
 Charging Dock Convenience 
 The charging dock enhances convenience by keeping the device powered and ready while also offering stable placement for easy access during busy operations. It helps reduce downtime and clutter, making it ideal for countertop use in fast-paced retail environments. 
 Security Features 
 The device is PCI PTS 5.x certified, ensuring it meets the highest industry standards for secure payment processing. It also features advanced encryption and secure boot technology to protect sensitive data and prevent unauthorized access.
