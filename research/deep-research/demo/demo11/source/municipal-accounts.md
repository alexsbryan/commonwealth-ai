# The municipal accounts

The tramway was financed by tramway stock: £214,000 issued at 3.5 per
cent at the opening, serviced from the undertaking's receipts.

Annual net receipts:

| Year | Gross receipts | Working expenses | Net receipts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1897 | £29,614 | £13,512 | £16,102 |
| 1901 | £34,208 | £14,324 | £19,884 |
| 1906 | £35,941 | £14,904 | £21,037 |

The 1901 accounts show the fare structure's arithmetic: 4,881,216
passengers produced £31,404 7s 6d of passenger receipts, supplemented
by £2,803 12s 6d of parcel and advertisement revenue, for gross
receipts of £34,208.

The undertaking first paid a dividend on the stock at the full 3.5 per
cent rate in 1898, and in 1906 transferred £4,000 to the town's
general rate fund — the first year the tramway contributed to the
rates.

Working expenses ran between 45.6 per cent of gross receipts (1897)
and 41.5 per cent (1906) — the fall reflecting the electrification's
lower haulage cost per car mile.
