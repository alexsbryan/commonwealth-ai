# Early ridership, 1896-1901

The tramway's passenger traffic in its first six years, from the
council's own returns:

| Year | Passengers | Car miles |
|---|---|---|
| 1896 (from 2 March) | 2,314,807 | 214,300 |
| 1897 | 3,401,115 | 298,440 |
| 1898 | 3,987,442 | 342,180 |
| 1899 | 4,316,088 | 371,940 |
| 1900 | 4,552,903 | 388,620 |
| 1901 | 4,881,216 | 412,400 |

The fares were a penny and twopenny stage system: 1d for a single
stage, 2d for the whole line. From May 1897 the council added a
halfpenny workmen's fare on the 06:10 and 07:10 departures from the
harbour terminus, which carried 1,146,092 workmen's rides in 1901.

Passenger receipts in 1901 were £31,404 7s 6d — the odd sixpence
arising from the halfpenny fares.

The opening year's figure of 2,314,807 passengers for ten months of
operation implies an annualised rate of about 2.78 million, a rate the
tramway passed within its first full year of operation.
