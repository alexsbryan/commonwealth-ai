The EU AI Act entered into force in phases to ensure that obligations land as institutional capacity and technology mature [Source: ev-1]. This deliberate schedule also serves as a compliance roadmap, exporting the "Brussels effect" to providers worldwide [Source: ev-1].

The specific deadlines and their requirements are structured by risk tier and system type:
*   **2025-02-02**: Prohibited practices (classified as "unacceptable risk") begin applying. These include social scoring and certain manipulative or exploitation-of-vulnerability uses [Source: ev-1].
*   **2025-08-02**: Obligations for general-purpose AI (GPAI) models take effect. These duties are scaled based on systemic-risk status, with a threshold of 10^26 FLOPs [Source: ev-1]. A GPAI Code of Practice was drafted between 2024 and 2025 to interpret these obligations [Source: ev-1].
*   **2026-08-02**: High-risk system obligations apply [Source: ev-1].
*   **2027-08-02**: A subset of high-risk system obligations applies from this date [Source: ev-1].

The Act was published in the EU Official Journal on 2024-07-12 and entered into force on 2024-18-01 (noted as 2024, 08, 01 in evidence) [Source: ev-1]. Implementation is led by the European AI Office, established in Brussels in 2024 [Source: ev-1].
