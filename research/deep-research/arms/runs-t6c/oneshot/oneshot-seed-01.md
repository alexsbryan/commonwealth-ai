Google announced its agreement to acquire the cloud-security firm Wiz on March 18, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The primary strategic reason for this acquisition was for Google Cloud, led by Thomas Kurian, to close a security gap with competitors AWS and Azure [Source: ev-1]. By pursuing Wiz, Google bet that cloud-security consolidation would become the next battleground in the "cloud wars" and identified AI-driven cloud adoption as the key demand driver for these services [Source: ev-1].

The deal signaled several important aspects about the cloud-security market and corporate valuation dynamics. First, it represented the largest acquisition in Google's history, valued at approximately $32 billion in an all-cash transaction [Source: ev-1]. Second, the final price of $32 billion was notably higher than Google’s earlier offer of $23 billion made in July 2024, which had been rejected by Wiz [Source: ev-1]. This increase suggests a significant escalation in competitive bidding or a reassessment of Wiz’s value following its initial rejection of the lower bid [Source: ev-1]. The transaction involved Wiz co-founder and CEO Assaf Rappaport as a named principal [Source: ev-1]. Although announced in March 2025, the deal did not close until after shareholder and regulatory steps were completed, with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
