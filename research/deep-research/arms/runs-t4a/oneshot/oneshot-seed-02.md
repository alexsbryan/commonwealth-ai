DeepSeek’s release of DeepSeek-R1 on January 20, 2025 [Source: ev-1] triggered the largest single-day loss in Nvidia's history because it demonstrated that a frontier-class reasoning model could be trained for a fraction of incumbent costs, thereby challenging the market thesis that AI capital expenditure is protected by compute scale [Source: ev-1]. Specifically, R1 was preceded by DeepSeek-V3 (released December 26, 2024), which reported training on roughly 2.79 million GPU-hours at a claimed cost of about US$5.6 million using export-restricted H800 chips [Source: ev-1]. This efficiency revealed that scarcity constraints—specifically US export controls forcing Chinese labs toward optimization rather than preference—drove the economics of frontier-AI training [Source: ev-1]. Consequently, Nvidia lost approximately US$589 billion in market capitalization on January 27, 2025, with about US$1 trillion erased across the tech complex as markets repriced this new economic reality [Source: ev-1].
