Google announced on March 18, 2025, that it had agreed to acquire cloud-security firm Wiz for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal [Source: ev-1]. This transaction represented the largest acquisition in Google's history and followed Wiz’s rejection of a previous $23 billion offer from Google in July 2024 [Source: ev-1].

The primary motivation for this acquisition was strategic; under Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud pursued Wiz to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure [Source: ev-1]. The move signaled that cloud-security consolidation is viewed as the next battleground in the "cloud wars," driven by increasing demand resulting from AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. Although announced in March 2025 after shareholder and regulatory steps were completed, reports indicated the deal's completion occurred in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
