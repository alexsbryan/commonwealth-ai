Anthropic released the Claude 4 family, consisting of two distinct models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The launch was structured to serve different market needs within a competitive landscape dominated by OpenAI’s o-series and an enterprise agentic-coding gold rush [Source: ev-1].

*   **Model Differentiation:** Anthropic positioned Opus 4 as its flagship model ("Claude 4") with pricing at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. In contrast, Sonnet 4 was released as the "fast workhorse" developer-default coding model, priced significantly lower at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].
*   **Capability Focus:** The headline capability for both models was agentic coding and computer use [Source: ev-1]. Specifically, Claude 4 introduced "computer use 2.0," which offered browser control that was roughly twice as reliable as the previous 3.x generation [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled Anthropic’s strategic positioning of Claude 4 as the safest and most reliable coding agent in the market [Source: ev-1]. This release was timed to coincide with the rise of Claude Code, which emerged as the defining agentic-coding product of 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The success of this strategy led to record API usage for Anthropic [Source: ev-1]. Furthermore, driven by the adoption of Claude Code following this launch trajectory, Anthropic raised a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion, cementing its position as the leader in coding agents [Source: ev-1].
