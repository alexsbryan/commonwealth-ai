The EU AI Act entered into force in phases between 2024 and 2027 because the phased entry was deliberate, designed so that obligations would land as institutional capacity and technology mature [Source: ev-1]. This schedule also serves to double as a compliance roadmap for providers worldwide through the "Brussels effect" [Source: ev-1].

The different deadlines require specific actions based on risk tiers and system types:
*   **Prohibited practices:** Obligations regarding "unacceptable risk" activities (such as social scoring) apply from February 2, 2025 [Source: ev-1].
*   **General-purpose AI (GPAI):** Model obligations begin applying from August 2, 2026? No, the text states GPAI model obligations are from August 2, **2025** [Source: ev-1]. These duties are scaled by systemic-risk status, utilizing a threshold of $10^{26}$ FLOPs [Source: ev-1].
*   **High-risk systems:** General high-risk system obligations apply from August 2, **2026**, with a specific subset of these obligations applying later on August 2, **2027** [Source: ev-1].

The Act was published in the EU Official Journal on July 12, **2024**, and entered into force on August 1, **2024** [Source: ev-1].
