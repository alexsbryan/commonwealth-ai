The EU AI Act entered into force in phases to ensure that obligations are implemented only as institutional capacity and technology mature, with the schedule also serving as a compliance roadmap for global providers [Source: ev-1].

The different deadlines require adherence to specific regulatory tiers based on risk levels:
*   **2025-02-02:** Prohibited practices (categorized as "unacceptable risk," such as social scoring) become applicable [Source: ev-1].
*   **2025-08-02:** Obligations regarding general-purpose AI (GPAI) models take effect [Source: ev-1]. These duties are scaled by systemic-risk status, utilizing a threshold of 10^26 FLOPs [Source: ev-1].
*   **2026-08-02:** High-risk system obligations apply to the majority of high-risk systems [Source: ev-1].
*   **2027-08-02:** A subset of remaining high-risk system obligations applies [Source: ev-1].
