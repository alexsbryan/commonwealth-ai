OpenAI released GPT-5 on August 7, 2025 [Source: ev-1], marking a strategic shift toward "give-away-the-frontier" pricing by making the model immediately available to free users [Source: ev-1]. This release fundamentally changed how OpenAI sells its models by consolidating its product lineup into a single unified system and eliminating the previous distinction between reasoning and non-reasoning products. Specifically, the separate o-series products (o3, o4-mini, o3-mini, and o3-pro) were retired and folded into GPT-5’s internal "reasoning modes," ending the need for customers to select from different specialized models [Source: ev-1].

The new sales approach is characterized by aggressive distribution on the free tier and significantly lower API costs compared to the previous generation. While Plus subscribers pay $20/month for unlimited access and Pro users pay $200/month, the API pricing was reduced to $1.25 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for GPT-5, with GPT-5 mini costing just $0.25 and $2 respectively [Source: ev-1]. This price collapse allowed GPT-5 to achieve a 78.6% score on SWE-bench Verified at a fraction of the cost of its predecessors [Source: ev-1]. By integrating features such as a 5-million-token context window, native image generation, deep research, file upload, and web search directly into one model, OpenAI moved the competitive focus from benchmark demonstrations to "default agency," prioritizing models that act autonomously over those that simply answer questions [Source: ev-1].
