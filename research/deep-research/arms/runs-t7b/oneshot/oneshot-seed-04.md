OpenAI released o3 and o4-mini on April 16, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The launch signaled that the direction of frontier reasoning models is shifting toward "tool-using reasoning at commodity prices" rather than just raw scale or cost [Source: ev-1].

Specifically, this release marked a strategic pivot in several ways:
*   **Native Integration:** It introduced vision built directly into the model itself (not bolted on) and native tool use (web search, code interpreter) trained into the architecture [Source: ev-1].
*   **Dynamic Compute:** It utilized "budget forcing," where a fixed maximum thought budget allows for task-adaptive compute, meaning harder questions trigger longer thinking times [Source: ev-1].
*   **Privacy & Policy:** OpenAI implemented its first private chain-of-thought policy, ensuring reasoning tokens are not exposed to API callers [Source: ev-1].
*   **Competitive Positioning:** With GPT-5 not yet shipping, OpenAI used this launch to defend the frontier against DeepSeek’s R1 cost shock and Anthropic’s agentic-coding push by demonstrating high capability at lower costs (o3 priced at $10/$40 per million input/output tokens; o4-mini at $1.10/$4.40) [Source: ev-1].

The release also highlighted a new performance benchmark, with o3 setting a record on ARC-AGI-1 of about 87.5% at high compute, reaching or exceeding the ~85% human baseline for the first time among models [Source: ev-1].
