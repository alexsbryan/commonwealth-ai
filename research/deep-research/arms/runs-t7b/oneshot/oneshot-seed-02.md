DeepSeek’s R1 release triggered the largest single-day loss in Nvidia's history because it demonstrated that a frontier-class, open-weights reasoning model could be trained for a fraction of the incumbent costs, thereby challenging the market thesis that AI capital expenditure and demand are protected by compute scale [Source: ev-1]. Specifically, on January 27, 2025, Nvidia lost approximately US$589 billion in market capitalization—the largest single-day loss in stock-market history at the time—with about US$1 trillion erased across the tech complex [Source: ev-1].

This event revealed several key aspects regarding frontier-AI training economics:
*   **Cost Efficiency:** The predecessor to R1, DeepSeek-V3 (released December 26, 2024), reported training on roughly 2.79 million GPU-hours at a claimed cost of only about US$5.6 million [Source: ev-1]. This low-cost capability was achieved using export-restricted H800 chips [Source: ev-1].
*   **Methodological Innovation:** R1’s reasoning capabilities were produced via reinforcement learning (GRPO), with the R1-Zero variant trained without any supervised fine-tuning [Source: ev-1].
*   **Driver of Economics:** The efficiency driving these lower costs was not due to preference but rather scarcity; US export controls restricting access to high-end hardware forced Chinese labs toward greater computational efficiency [Source: ev-1].
