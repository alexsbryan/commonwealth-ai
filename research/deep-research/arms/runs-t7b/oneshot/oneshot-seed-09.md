Anthropic released the Claude 4 family, consisting of two distinct models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The decision to release them as a dual-model lineup was driven by specific market positioning and pricing strategies. Opus 4 served as the flagship model with higher costs at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. In contrast, Sonnet 4 was priced significantly lower at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, establishing it as the developer-default coding model for broader adoption [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that Anthropic viewed agentic coding and computer use as critical competitive frontiers in 2025, particularly against OpenAI’s o-series models [Source: ev-1]. By introducing "computer use 2.0," which offered browser control roughly twice as reliable as the previous 3.x generation, Anthropic positioned Claude 4 as the safest and most reliable coding agent available [Source: ev-1]. This strategy was timed to coincide with the rise of Claude Code, a defining product of the enterprise agentic-coding gold rush, leading to record API usage for Anthropic [Source: ev-1]. The market impact was substantial; driven by this adoption, Anthropic secured a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion, cementing its status as a leader in the coding-agent sector [Source: ev-1].
