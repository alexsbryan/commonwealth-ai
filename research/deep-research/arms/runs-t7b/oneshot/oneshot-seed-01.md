Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz to close a security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, positioning itself for consolidation in the sector driven by AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled that cloud-security is becoming the next major battleground in the "cloud wars," where companies are betting on consolidation as a key strategy [Source: ev-1].

Key details of this strategic move include:
*   **Deal Value:** Google agreed to pay approximately $32 billion in an all-cash transaction, which stands as the largest acquisition in its history [Source: ev-1].
*   **Negotiation History:** This final price followed Wiz's rejection of an earlier offer from Google worth $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1].
*   **Timeline:** The agreement was announced on March 18, 2025 [Source: ev-1], and after completing shareholder and regulatory steps, the deal officially closed in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
