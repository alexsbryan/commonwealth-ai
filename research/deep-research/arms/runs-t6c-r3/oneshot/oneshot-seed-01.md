Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz in March 2025 to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, positioning itself for the next battleground of the "cloud wars" [Source: ev-1]. The company bet on cloud-security consolidation as a strategic priority and identified AI-driven cloud adoption as the primary demand driver for this sector [Source: ev-1].

The deal signaled that major tech firms view cloud-security consolidation as critical to their competitive standing. This was underscored by the transaction's scale—an all-cash purchase worth approximately $32 billion, making it Google’s largest acquisition ever—and the fact that it followed Wiz’s rejection of an earlier $23 billion offer in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. Although announced on March 18, 2025, the deal ultimately closed after shareholder and regulatory steps were completed later in 2025 [Source: ev-1].
