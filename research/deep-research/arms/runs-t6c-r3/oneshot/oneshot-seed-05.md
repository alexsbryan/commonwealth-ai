The EU AI Act entered into force in phases between 2024 and 2027 because the phased entry was deliberate, designed so that obligations would land as institutional capacity and technology mature. Additionally, this schedule serves as a compliance roadmap for providers worldwide [Source: ev-1].

*   **August 2, 2026:** Obligations for high-risk AI systems apply [Source: ev-1].
