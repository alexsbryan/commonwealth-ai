Anthropic released Claude 4 as two distinct models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This dual-model strategy was designed to address different market needs within the agentic-coding sector. The flagship model, Opus 4 (referred to as "Claude 4"), was positioned for high-reliability tasks with pricing set at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. In contrast, Sonnet 4 served as a fast workhorse and developer-default coding model, offering significantly lower costs of $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that Anthropic intended to lead in the enterprise agentic-coding gold rush by positioning its models as the safest and most reliable coding agents against competitors like OpenAI’s o-series [Source: ev-1]. A key signal regarding market capabilities was the introduction of "computer use 2.0," which allowed for browser control with roughly twice the reliability of the previous 3.x generation [Source: ev-1]. The timing of this release coincided with the rise of Claude Code, identified as a defining product for agentic coding in 2025, driving record API usage [Source: ev-1].

This strategic focus on agentic coding had significant financial implications; driven by adoption rates from these tools, Anthropic raised a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion, cementing its position as a leader in the market [Source: ev-1].
