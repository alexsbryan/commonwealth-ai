Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, positioning itself for consolidation in the cloud-security sector. The acquisition was driven by Google Cloud’s strategy under Thomas Kurian, which bet on AI-driven cloud adoption as a key demand driver [Source: ev-1].

The deal signaled that cloud-security consolidation is becoming the next major battleground in the "cloud wars" [Source: ev-1]. Announced on March 18, 2025, the transaction valued Wiz at approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal, marking it as the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This final price followed Wiz’s rejection of an earlier offer from Google for $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. The agreement was finalized after completing necessary shareholder and regulatory steps, with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
