Google announced on March 18, 2025 [Source: ev-1], that it had agreed to acquire cloud-security firm Wiz for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal [Source: ev-1]. This transaction represented the largest acquisition in Google's history and followed Wiz’s rejection of a previous $23 billion offer from Google in July 2024 [Source: ev-1].

The primary reason for the acquisition was strategic; under Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud pursued Wiz specifically to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled that cloud-security consolidation is emerging as the next major battleground in the "cloud wars" [Source: ev-1]. Furthermore, it indicated that AI-driven cloud adoption would serve as a key demand driver for these services [Source: ev-1]. Although announced in March 2025, the deal officially closed after completing shareholder and regulatory steps, with completion reported in July 2025 [ev-1].
