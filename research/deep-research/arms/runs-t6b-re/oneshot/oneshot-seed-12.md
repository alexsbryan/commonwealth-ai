Microsoft's Recall feature became a privacy scandal in 2024 because, despite claims that data would be encrypted, security researchers demonstrated that screenshots were stored unencrypted (in plaintext) within a local SQLite database [Source: ev-1]. This allowed any process running as the user to access sensitive information captured by default on screen, including passwords and other private content [Source: ev-1]. The feature was originally announced on May 20, 2024, at the Copilot+ PC launch event [Source: ev-1].

In response to the controversy, Microsoft delayed the release of Recall in June 2024 and subsequently reversed its design approach [Source: ev-1]. The relaunched feature is now opt-in (default OFF), requires Windows Hello authentication and presence detection to open the timeline, and excludes incognito or DRM-protected content from capture [Source: ev-1]. This revised version reached general availability on Copilot+ PCs in 2025 [Source: ev-1].
