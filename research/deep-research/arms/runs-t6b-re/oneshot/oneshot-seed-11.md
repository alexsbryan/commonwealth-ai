Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on July 9, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by several key factors related to AI infrastructure spending and demand:

*   **Accelerator Demand:** There was strong demand for Blackwell accelerator systems, specifically the GB200/GB300 rack systems [Source: ev-1].
*   **Major Infrastructure Projects:** The Stargate project involved a ~$500 billion plan for AI infrastructure [Source: ev-1].
*   **Hyperscaler Spending:** Major technology companies (Microsoft, Meta, Google, Amazon) repeatedly raised their capital expenditure guidance through 2025, with Nvidia holding a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1].
*   **Shift to Inference:** CEO Jensen Huang framed this period as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" that sell compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].

This milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, viewing Nvidia as its primary beneficiary or "toll-booth" [Source: ev-1]. The rapid growth trajectory—from $1 trillion in mid-2023, to $2 trillion and $3 trillion in 2024, and finally $4 trillion in July 2025—underscores the scale of this spending supercycle [Source: ev-1].
