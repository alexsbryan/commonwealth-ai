Anthropic released the "Claude 4" family, consisting of two models—Opus 4 and Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The launch signaled a shift toward agentic coding and computer use as defining capabilities for AI products in 2025, positioning Claude 4 as the safest and most reliable coding agent against competitors like OpenAI's o-series during an enterprise agentic-coding gold rush [Source: ev-1].

The release featured specific pricing structures to target different user needs: Opus 4 was priced at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens, while Sonnet 4 was set at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. This structure positioned Sonnet 4 as the developer-default coding model [Source: ev-1]. The headline capability of this launch was "computer use 2.0," which offered browser control roughly twice as reliable as the previous 3.x generation models [Source: ev-1].

This strategic timing aligned with the rise of Claude Code as a defining agentic-coding product in 2025 and led to record API usage for Anthropic [Source: ev-1]. Following an update on June 18, 2025 (version 4.1), which improved coding reliability at the same prices, Anthropic raised a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of $183 billion, cementing its position as the leader in the coding-agent market [Source: ev-1].
