Anthropic released the Claude 4 family on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This release consisted of two distinct models to address different market needs and price points. The first model was Opus 4 (referred to as "Claude 4"), which served as the flagship product with pricing set at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. The second model was Sonnet 4, positioned as a fast workhorse and developer-default coding model, priced significantly lower at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that agentic coding had become a defining focus for AI products in 2025, specifically timed to coincide with the rise of Claude Code as the leading agentic-coding tool [Source: ev-1]. By highlighting "computer use 2.0" (browser control) which was roughly twice as reliable as the previous generation's capabilities, Anthropic emphasized reliability over raw speed or cost alone [Source: ev-1]. This strategy positioned Claude 4 against competitors like OpenAI’s o-series during an enterprise "agentic-coding gold rush," aiming to establish itself as the safest and most reliable coding agent available [Source: ev-1]. The market response validated this positioning; driven by strong API usage and adoption of Claude Code, Anthropic secured a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion, cementing its status as the leader in the coding-agent sector [Source: ev-1].
