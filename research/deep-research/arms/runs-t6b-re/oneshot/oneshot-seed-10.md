OpenAI released GPT-5 on August 7, 2025 [ev-1] to consolidate its product strategy around a single unified model and shift the competitive focus from benchmark demonstrations to "default agency," where models act rather than just answer [ev-1]. This release marked a deliberate strategic pivot toward aggressive distribution by making the frontier model available immediately to free users [ev-1].

This launch fundamentally changed how OpenAI sells its models in several ways:
*   **Pricing Model:** It introduced a "give-away-the-frontier" approach with a free tier, alongside paid tiers of $20/month for Plus (offering unlimited GPT-5) and $200/month for Pro [ev-1]. API pricing was set at $1.25 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for GPT-5, while GPT-5 mini costs $0.25 and $2 respectively [ev-1]. These prices were significantly lower than the previous o-series products [ev-1].
*   **Product Consolidation:** OpenAI retired separate reasoning models like o3, o4-mini, o3-mini, and o3-pro, folding their capabilities into GPT-5’s internal "reasoning modes" [ev-1]. This ended the distinction between reasoning and non-reasoning models, removing the need for users to select different model types [ev-1].
*   **Feature Integration:** The new sales pitch emphasizes a comprehensive suite of integrated features within one model, including a 5-million-token context window, native image generation (eliminating the need for a separate image model), deep research integration, file upload, and web search [ev-1].

Despite these changes in pricing and packaging, GPT-5 maintained high performance, scoring approximately 78.6% on SWE-bench Verified [ev-1], with GPT-5 mini performing closely behind at a fraction of the previous costs [ev-1].
