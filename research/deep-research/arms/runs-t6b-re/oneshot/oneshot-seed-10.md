OpenAI released GPT-5 on August 7, 2025 [Source: ev-1], marking a strategic pivot to consolidate its product lineup and shift the competitive focus from benchmark demonstrations to "default agency" (models that act rather than just answer) [Source: ev-1]. The release was designed to end the previous distinction between reasoning and non-reasoning models by folding separate products like o3, o4-mini, o3-mini, and o3-pro into unified internal "reasoning modes" within a single model architecture [Source: ev-1].

This launch fundamentally changed how OpenAI sells its models through aggressive pricing and distribution strategies. Specifically, it adopted a "give-away-the-frontier" approach by making GPT-5 immediately available to free users on the free tier [Source: ev-1]. While paid tiers remain at $20/month for Plus (offering unlimited access) and $200/month for Pro, API pricing was significantly reduced compared to previous offerings, costing $1.25 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens for GPT-5, with GPT-5 mini priced even lower at $0.25 and $2 respectively [Source: ev-1]. This consolidation allowed OpenAI to offer advanced capabilities—including a 5-million-token context window, native image generation, deep research integration, file upload, and web search—across all users without requiring customers to select between different model variants [Source: ev-1].
