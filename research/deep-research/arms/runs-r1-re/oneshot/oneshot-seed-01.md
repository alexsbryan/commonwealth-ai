Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal, a transaction announced on March 18, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This acquisition was driven by Google Cloud’s strategy to close the security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, betting that cloud-security consolidation would become the next battleground in the "cloud wars" while leveraging AI-driven cloud adoption as a key demand driver [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled a major shift toward consolidating the market around specialized security capabilities within broader cloud ecosystems.

This agreement represented the largest acquisition in Google's history, following Wiz's earlier rejection of a $23 billion offer from Google in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. Although announced in March 2025, the transaction completed its shareholder and regulatory steps later in the year, with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
