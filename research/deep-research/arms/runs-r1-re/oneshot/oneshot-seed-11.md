Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on 2025-07-09, making it the first company in history to close above this threshold [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by several key factors:

*   **Product Demand:** Strong demand for Blackwell accelerator demand (GB200/GB300 rack systems) [Source: ev-1].
*   **Major Projects and Capex:** The Stargate project's ~$500B AI-infrastructure plan and repeatedly raised hyperscaler capex guidance through 2025 from Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon [Source: ev-1].
*   **Market Position:** Nvidia holds a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1].
*   **Strategic Shift:** CEO Jensen Huang framed this moment as the transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" selling compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].

The milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet, with Nvidia serving as its "toll-booth" for this spending supercycle [Source: ev-1]. This rapid growth followed a trajectory where Nvidia reached $1T in mid-2023, $2T in Feb 2024, and $3T in June 2024 before hitting $4T in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
