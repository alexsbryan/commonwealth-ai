Anthropic released the Claude 4 family, consisting of two distinct models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The decision to launch two models was driven by a strategic positioning within the enterprise agentic-coding market. Opus 4 served as the flagship model ("Claude 4"), while Sonnet 4 acted as the "fast workhorse" and developer-default coding model [Source: ev-1]. This dual-model approach allowed Anthropic to offer different price points tailored to specific use cases: Opus 4 was priced at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens, whereas Sonnet 4 was significantly cheaper at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that agentic coding had become a defining product category for 2025, with Claude Code emerging as its centerpiece [Source: ev-1]. By introducing "computer use 2.0," which offered roughly twice the reliability of the previous 3.x generation in browser control tasks, Anthropic positioned itself against competitors like OpenAI's o-series during an enterprise gold rush [Source: ev-1]. The release demonstrated that high-reliability agentic capabilities were critical market differentiators; this was further validated when Anthropic reported record API usage and subsequently raised funding at a valuation of approximately $183 billion by late September 2025, cementing its status as a leader in coding agents [Source: ev-1].
