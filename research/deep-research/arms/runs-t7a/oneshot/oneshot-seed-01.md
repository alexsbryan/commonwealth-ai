Google announced on March 18, 2025 [ev-1], that it had agreed to acquire cloud-security firm Wiz for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal [Source: ev-1]. This transaction represented the largest acquisition in Google's history and followed Wiz’s rejection of a previous offer from Google worth $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1].

The primary reason for the acquisition was strategic competition within the "cloud wars." Google Cloud, led by Thomas Kurian, pursued Wiz specifically to close its security gap compared to competitors AWS and Azure [Source: ev-1]. By acquiring Wiz, Google bet on two key market trends: first, that cloud-security consolidation would become the next major battleground in the industry; second, that AI-driven cloud adoption would serve as the primary demand driver for these services [Source: ev-1]. Consequently, the deal signaled that securing a dominant position in integrated cloud security is now viewed as essential for maintaining competitiveness against other major hyperscalers. The deal closed after shareholder and regulatory approvals, with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
