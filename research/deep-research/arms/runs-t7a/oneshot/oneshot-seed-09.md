Anthropic released the Claude 4 family, consisting of two distinct models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This dual-model strategy was designed to serve different market segments with specific pricing structures: Opus 4 served as the flagship model priced at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens, while Sonnet 4 acted as a fast workhorse and developer-default coding model priced at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that Anthropic positioned itself as the leader in agentic-coding reliability during a period of intense competition against OpenAI’s o-series models [Source: ev-1]. By introducing "computer use 2.0" features for browser control that were roughly twice as reliable as those in the previous generation (the 3.x series), Anthropic demonstrated its focus on safe and reliable autonomous agents [Source: ev-1]. This strategic emphasis on agentic capabilities, driven by the adoption of Claude Code, cemented Anthropic's position in the market and contributed to it raising a large funding round at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion in late September 2025 [Source: ev-1].
