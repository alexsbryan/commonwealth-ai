Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on July 9, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by several key factors related to artificial intelligence infrastructure spending and demand.

First, there was strong demand for Blackwell accelerator systems, specifically the GB200/GB300 rack systems [Source: ev-1]. Second, major AI-infrastructure projects contributed significantly, such as the Stargate project with a plan worth approximately $500 billion [Source: ev-1]. Third, hyperscalers including Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon repeatedly raised their capital expenditure (capex) guidance throughout 2025, signaling sustained investment in this sector [Source: ev-1]. During this period, Nvidia maintained a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1].

This milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era [Source: ev-1]. It reflects an "AI-capex supercycle" where compute is increasingly viewed as a commodity sold by "AI factories," marking a transition from training to inference demand as framed by CEO Jensen Huang [Source: ev-1]. The rapid growth trajectory leading up to this point included reaching $1 trillion in mid-2023, $2 trillion in February 2024, and $3 trillion in June 2024 before hitting $4 trillion in July 2025 following a multi-hundred-billion-dollar run in early July 2025 when its share price crossed about $160 [Source: ev-1].
