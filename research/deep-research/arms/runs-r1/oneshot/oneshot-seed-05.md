The EU AI Act entered into force in phases to ensure that obligations are implemented as institutional capacity and technology mature, with the schedule also serving as a compliance roadmap for global providers [Source: ev-1].

The different deadlines require the following specific actions:
*   **2025-02-02**: Prohibited practices (categorized as "unacceptable risk") begin applying [Source: ev-1].
*   **2025-08-02**: Obligations regarding general-purpose AI (GPAI) models take effect [Source: ev-1].
*   **2026-08-02**: High-risk system obligations apply to most systems [Source: ev-1].
*   **2027-08-02**: A subset of high-risk system obligations applies [Source: ev-1].
