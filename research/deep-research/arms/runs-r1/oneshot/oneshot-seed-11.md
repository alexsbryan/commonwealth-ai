Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on July 9, 2025 [Source: ev-1], making it the first company in history to close above this threshold after Apple touched $4 trillion intraday on July 8, 2025. This milestone was driven by several key factors:

*   **Product Demand:** Strong demand for Blackwell accelerator GB200/GB300 rack systems [Source: ev-1].
*   **Major Projects and Capex:** The Stargate project's ~$500 billion AI-infrastructure plan and repeatedly raised capital expenditure guidance from hyperscalers like Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon throughout 2025.
*   **Market Position:** Nvidia’s dominant share of the AI-accelerator market.
*   **Strategic Shift:** CEO Jensen Huang framed this period as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" that sell compute as a commodity.

The $4 trillion milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era [Source: ev-1]. It reflects an ongoing AI-capex supercycle where Nvidia acts as the primary beneficiary or "toll-booth." The rapid trajectory—from $1 trillion in mid-2023 to $2 trillion in February 2024, $3 trillion in June 2024, and finally $4 trillion in July 2025—underscores the scale of this spending surge [Source: ev-1].
