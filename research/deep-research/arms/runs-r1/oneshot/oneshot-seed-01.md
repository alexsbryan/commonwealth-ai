Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, betting that cloud-security consolidation would be the next battleground in the "cloud wars" driven by AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled a major shift toward consolidating the cloud-security market as a critical component of competing for enterprise customers relying on AI infrastructure.

The acquisition was announced on March 18, 2025, for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash transaction, marking the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This final price followed Wiz’s rejection of an earlier offer from Google worth $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. Although the announcement occurred in March 2025 and regulatory steps were required, the deal officially closed after shareholder approval with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
