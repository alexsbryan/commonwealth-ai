DeepSeek’s release of the R1 model on 2025-01-20 triggered the largest single-day loss in Nvidia's history because it demonstrated that frontier-class reasoning models could be trained for a fraction of incumbent costs, challenging the market thesis that AI capital expenditure and demand were moated by compute scale [Source: ev-1]. This event revealed that US export controls, specifically restrictions on H800 chips, had forced Chinese labs to prioritize efficiency rather than raw compute preference, fundamentally altering the economics of frontier-AI training [Source: ev-1].

The evidence supports these conclusions with specific figures indicating that DeepSeek’s predecessor, V3 (released 2024-12-26), was trained using roughly 2.79 million GPU-hours at a claimed cost of about US$5.6 million on restricted hardware [Source: ev-1]. Following R1's release, Nvidia lost approximately US$589 billion in market capitalization on 2025-1/27, while an additional amount totaling $1 trillion was erased across the broader tech complex [Source: ev-1].
