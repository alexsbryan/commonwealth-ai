Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz to close a security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, betting on cloud-security consolidation as the next battleground in the "cloud wars" and on AI-driven cloud adoption as a key demand driver [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled that major tech companies view securing cloud infrastructure—particularly amid increased AI usage—as a critical strategic priority requiring significant investment through large-scale acquisitions.

The acquisition was announced on March 18, 2025, for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash transaction, which marked the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This final price followed Wiz’s rejection of an earlier offer from Google worth $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. Although the announcement occurred in early 2025 and the deal closed after regulatory steps with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1], it represented a major consolidation move by Google Cloud under Thomas Kurian to strengthen its security offerings.
