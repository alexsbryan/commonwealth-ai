Anthropic released the "Claude 4" family in May [2025] as two distinct models to address different performance and cost requirements within its agentic-coding strategy. The flagship model, Opus 4, was positioned for maximum capability at a price of $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens. In contrast, Sonnet 4 served as the fast workhorse and developer-default coding model, priced significantly lower at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens. This dual-model approach allowed Anthropic to offer both high-end reliability and accessible speed for developers.

The launch signaled that the market had shifted toward "agentic coding" as the defining product category of [2025], driven by tools like Claude Code. By emphasizing that its new "computer use 2.0" was roughly twice as reliable as the previous generation's capabilities (referenced in context with version numbers such as 3.x), Anthropic positioned itself against competitors like OpenAI’s o-series during an enterprise agentic-coding gold rush. The release highlighted a focus on safety and reliability, which contributed to record API usage and ultimately cemented Anthropic’s position as a leader in this sector, leading to a large funding round at a reported ~$183 billion valuation later in [2025].
