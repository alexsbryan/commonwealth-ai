Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on 2025-07-09, making it the first company in history to close above this valuation [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by strong demand for Blackwell accelerator rack systems (specifically GB200/GB300), the Stargate project's ~$500B AI-infrastructure plan, and repeatedly raised capital expenditure guidance from major hyperscalers including Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon throughout 2025 [Source: ev-1]. At the time of crossing the threshold, Nvidia’s share price had reached approximately $160 [Source: ev-1].

The trajectory leading up to this event saw Nvidia reach a $1 trillion market cap in mid-2023, $2 trillion in February 2024, and $3 trillion in June 2024 before hitting $4 trillion in July 2025 following a multi-hundred-billion-dollar run earlier that month [Source: ev-1]. This milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, with Nvidia positioned as its "toll-booth" due to its dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1]. CEO Jensen Huang framed this moment as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" selling compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].
