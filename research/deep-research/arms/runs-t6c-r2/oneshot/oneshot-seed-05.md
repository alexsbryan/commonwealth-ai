The EU AI Act entered into force in phases to ensure that obligations are implemented only as institutional capacity and technology mature [Source: ev-1]. This deliberate schedule also serves as a compliance roadmap, exporting the "Brussels effect" to providers worldwide [Source: ev-1].

Regarding the specific deadlines and requirements between 2024 and 2027:
*   **July 12, 2024**: The law was published in the EU Official Journal [Source: ev-1].
*   **August 1, 2024**: The Act officially entered into force [Source: ev-1].
*   **February 2, 2025**: Prohibited practices (categorized as "unacceptable risk") become applicable. These include social scoring and certain manipulative or exploitation-of-vulnerability uses [Source: ev-1].
*   **August 2, 2026**: Obligations for high-risk AI systems begin to apply [Source: ev-1].
*   **August 2, 207**: A subset of high-risk system obligations applies from this date [Source: ev-1].

Additionally, the evidence notes that general-purpose AI (GPAI) model obligations are scheduled to start on August 2, 205 [Source: ev-1]. The European AI Office was established in 24 to lead implementation, and a GPAI Code of Practice was drafted through 204–205 with broad industry participation [Source: ev-1].
