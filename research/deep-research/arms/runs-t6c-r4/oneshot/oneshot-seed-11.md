Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on July 9, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by strong demand for Blackwell accelerator GB200/GB300 rack systems and the Stargate project's approximately $500 billion AI-infrastructure plan [Source: ev-1]. Additionally, hyperscalers including Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon repeatedly raised their capital expenditure guidance throughout 2025, while Nvidia maintained a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1].

The trajectory to this valuation saw Nvidia reach a $1 trillion market cap in mid-2023, $2 trillion in February 2024, and $3 trillion in June 2024 before hitting the $4 trillion mark in July 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, with Nvidia positioned as its "toll-booth" [Source: ev-1]. CEO Jensen Huang framed this moment as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" selling compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].
