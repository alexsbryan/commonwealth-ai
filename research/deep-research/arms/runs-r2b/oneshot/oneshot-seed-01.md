Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz in March 2025 to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, positioning itself for what it viewed as the next battleground of the "cloud wars" [Source: ev-1]. The company bet on cloud-security consolidation and identified AI-driven cloud adoption as a key demand driver for this move [Source: ev-1].

The deal signaled that major tech firms are prioritizing strategic consolidation in the cloud-security market to secure competitive advantages. This was underscored by the transaction being Google's largest acquisition ever, valued at approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal announced on March 18, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. Following Wiz’s rejection of an earlier $23 billion offer in July 2024, the final agreement highlighted the high value placed on security assets, with the deal closing after regulatory steps and completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
