Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on July 9, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by several key factors related to the AI infrastructure supercycle:

*   **Product Demand and Projects:** Strong demand for Blackwell accelerator GB200/GB300 rack systems and the Stargate project’s approximately $500 billion AI-infrastructure plan were primary drivers [Source: ev-1].
*   **Hyperscaler Spending:** Major hyperscalers (Microsoft, Meta, Google, Amazon) repeatedly raised their capital expenditure guidance through 2025, with Nvidia holding a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1].
*   **Market Shift:** CEO Jensen Huang characterized this period as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" that sell compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].

The milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, positioning Nvidia as its central beneficiary or "toll-booth" [Source: ev-1]. This follows a rapid trajectory where Nvidia reached $1 trillion in mid-2023, $2 trillion in February 2024, and $3 trillion in June 2024 before hitting $4 trillion in July 2025 following a multi-hundred-billion-dollar run earlier in July 2025 when its share price crossed about $160 [Source: ev-1].
