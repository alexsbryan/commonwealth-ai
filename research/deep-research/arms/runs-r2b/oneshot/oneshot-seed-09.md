Anthropic released the Claude 4 family, consisting of two distinct models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The decision to release them as a pair was driven by specific market positioning and pricing strategies. Claude Opus 4 served as the flagship model, priced at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. In contrast, Claude Sonnet 4 was positioned as the "fast workhorse" and developer-default coding model, with significantly lower pricing at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [ev-1] [Source: ev-1].

This dual-model launch signaled several key trends in the agentic-coding market:
*   **Reliability as a Core Value:** The headline capability for both models was agentic coding and computer use. Specifically, Claude 4's "computer use 2.0" (browser control) was roughly twice as reliable as the previous 3.x generation [Source: ev-1]. This highlighted that reliability had become a critical differentiator in this sector.
*   **Competitive Positioning Against OpenAI:** The launch occurred against the backdrop of the OpenAI o-series and an enterprise "agentic-coding gold rush." Anthropic explicitly positioned the Claude 4 family as the safest and most reliable coding agent to compete effectively in this space [Source: ev-1].
*   **Rise of Agentic Products:** The timing coincided with the rise of Claude Code, which emerged as the defining agentic-coding product of 2025, indicating a shift toward more autonomous software development tools [Source: ev-1].

The market's response validated these signals. Driven by strong adoption of Claude Code and record API usage, Anthropic secured a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion, cementing its position as the leader in the coding-agent market [Source: ev-1].
