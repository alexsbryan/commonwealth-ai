Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz in an all-cash deal valued at approximately $32 billion, which was announced on 03/18/2025 and became the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This transaction followed Wiz’s rejection of a previous offer from Google worth $23 billion in 2024 [Source: ev-1].

The primary motivation for the acquisition was to close the security gap with competitors AWS and Azure under the leadership of Thomas Kurian at Google Cloud [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled that cloud-security consolidation is viewed as the next major battleground in the "cloud wars," driven by increasing demand resulting from AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. Although announced in March, the deal completed its shareholder and regulatory steps later, with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
