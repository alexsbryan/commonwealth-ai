Google announced on March 18, 2025 [ev-1], that it had agreed to acquire the cloud-security firm Wiz for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal [Source: ev-1]. This transaction represented the largest acquisition in Google's history and followed a previous negotiation where Wiz rejected Google’s earlier offer of $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1].

The primary motivation for this acquisition was strategic; under Thomas Kurian, Google Cloud sought to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure [Source: ev-1]. The move signaled that cloud-security consolidation is viewed as the next major battleground in the "cloud wars," driven by increasing demand from AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. Although announced in March 2025, the deal officially closed after completing shareholder and regulatory steps, with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
