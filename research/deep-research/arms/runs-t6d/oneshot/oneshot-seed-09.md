Anthropic released the Claude 4 family, consisting of two models—Claude Opus 4 and Claude Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This dual-model strategy was designed to address different market needs by positioning Opus 4 as the flagship model with pricing at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens, while Sonnet 4 served as a "fast workhorse" and developer-default coding model priced significantly lower at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that Anthropic had positioned itself as the leader in the agentic-coding market, specifically emphasizing reliability over raw speed or cost alone. A key capability driving this signal was "computer use 2.0," which offered browser control roughly twice as reliable as the previous generation's features [Source: ev-1]. By timing the release to coincide with the rise of Claude Code—the defining agentic-coding product of 2025—Anthropic directly challenged competitors like OpenAI’s o-series during an enterprise "agentic-coding gold rush" [Source: ev-1]. The strategy proved successful, leading to record API usage and a large funding round in late September 2025 at a reported valuation of approximately $183 billion, cementing Anthropic’s position as the coding-agent leader [Source: ev-1].
