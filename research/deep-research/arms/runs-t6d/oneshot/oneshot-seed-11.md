Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on 2025-07-09, making it the first company in history to close above that threshold [Source: ev-1]. This milestone was driven by several key factors:

*   **Product Demand:** Strong demand for Blackwell accelerator demand (GB200/GB300 rack systems) [Source: ev-1].
*   **Major Projects & Capex:** The Stargate project's ~$500B AI-infrastructure plan and repeatedly raised hyperscaler capex guidance from Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon through 2025 [Source: ev-1].
*   **Market Position:** Nvidia holds a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [Source: ev-1].
*   **Shift in Usage:** CEO Jensen Huang framed the moment as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" selling compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].

The trajectory leading up to this milestone was rapid: $1T (mid-2023), $2T (Feb 2024), $3T (June 2024), and finally $4T (July 2025) [Source: ev-1]. This included a multi-hundred-billion-dollar run in the first half of July 2025, with Nvidia's share price crossing about $160 [Source: ev-1].

This milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, viewing Nvidia as its "toll-booth" for this spending supercycle [Source: ev-1]. However, it also highlights risk due to concentration, as Nvidia’s weight in the S&P 500 reached record levels, and membership in the $4T club rotated among Nvidia, Apple (which touched $4T intraday on 2025-7-8), and Microsoft through 2025.
