OpenAI released o3 and o4-mini on April 16, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The launch signaled a strategic direction for frontier reasoning models characterized by the integration of vision and native tool use directly into the model architecture rather than bolting them on [Source: ev-1]. It also marked OpenAI’s first implementation of a private chain-of-thought policy, where reasoning tokens are not exposed to API callers [Source: ev-1].

The release was designed to defend the reasoning-model frontier against cost pressures from DeepSeek's R1 and competitive agentic-coding efforts by Anthropic [Source: ev-1]. By offering o3 at $10 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, and o4-mini at $1.10 per million input tokens and 4.40 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1], OpenAI reframed frontier capability as tool-using reasoning available at commodity prices [Source: ev-1]. Furthermore, with GPT-5 not yet shipping, these models served to maintain leadership in the space [Source: ev-1]. The launch also highlighted significant performance gains, such as o3 achieving about 87.5% on ARC-AGI-1, which met or exceeded the ~85% human baseline [Source: ev-1].
