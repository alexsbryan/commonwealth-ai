Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz, announcing the agreement on March 18, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The transaction was valued at approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal, which marked the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This final price followed Wiz’s rejection of a previous offer from Google for $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1].

The primary motivation for the acquisition was to close the security gap with competitors AWS and Azure under the leadership of Thomas Kurian at Google Cloud [Source: ev-1]. By pursuing this deal, Google signaled that it viewed cloud-security consolidation as the next major battleground in the "cloud wars" [Source: ev-1]. Additionally, the move indicated a strategic bet on AI-driven cloud adoption serving as the key demand driver for these services [Source: ev-1]. The deal ultimately closed after completing shareholder and regulatory steps, with completion reported in July 2025 [ev-1].
