Anthropic released the "Claude 4" family, consisting of two models—Opus 4 and Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The decision to launch these as distinct tiers was driven by a specific pricing and positioning strategy: Opus 4 served as the flagship model with higher costs ($15 per million input tokens / $75 per million output tokens), while Sonnet 4 acted as the fast workhorse priced at $3 per million input tokens / $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. This structure allowed Anthropic to position Sonnet specifically as the developer-default coding model [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that agentic coding had become the defining capability of the market, particularly through the rise of Claude Code in 2025 [Source: ev-1]. By introducing "computer use 2.0" (browser control) which was roughly twice as reliable as the previous generation's features, Anthropic positioned itself against competitors like OpenAI’s o-series and the broader enterprise agentic-coding gold rush by offering what it claimed were the safest and most reliable coding agents [Source: ev-1]. This strategic focus on reliability and agentic capabilities directly contributed to record API usage and ultimately cemented Anthropic’s position as a leader in the sector, leading to a large funding round at a reported ~$183 billion valuation later that year [Source: ev-1].
