Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on July 9, 2025 [Source: ev-1], making it the first company in history to close above that threshold after Apple touched $4 trillion intraday on July 8, 2025 [ev-1]. This milestone was driven by strong demand for Blackwell accelerator rack systems (GB200/GB300), the Stargate project's ~$500 billion AI-infrastructure plan, and repeatedly raised capital expenditure guidance from hyperscalers such as Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon throughout 2025, all while Nvidia maintained a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market [ev-1]. The event reflects an "AI-capex supercycle" where investors priced AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, positioning Nvidia as its primary beneficiary or "toll-booth," with CEO Jensen Huang framing the shift toward inference demand and compute sold as a commodity via "AI factories" [Source: ev-1].
