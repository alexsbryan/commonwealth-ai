The EU AI Act entered into force in phases between 2024 and 2027 because the phased entry was deliberate, designed so that obligations land as institutional capacity and technology mature [Source: ev-1]. This schedule also serves as a compliance roadmap for the "Brussels effect," which exports these standards to providers worldwide [Source: ev-1].

The specific deadlines and their requirements are as follows:
*   **August 1, 2024:** The law officially entered into force after being published on July 12, 2024 [Source: ev-1].
*   **February 2, 2025:** Prohibited practices (classified as unacceptable risk) apply. These include social scoring and certain manipulative or exploitation-of-vulnerability uses [Source: ev-1].
*   **August 2, 20 GPAI model obligations begin applying, with duties scaled by systemic-risk status based on a threshold of $10^{26}$ FLOPs [Source: ev-1].
*   **August 2, 206:** High-risk system obligations become applicable [Source: ev-1].
*   **August 2, 7:** A subset of high-risk system obligations applies [Source: ev-1].

Additionally, the European AI Office leads implementation in Brussels, and fines for prohibited-practice violations reach €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover (whichever is higher), while most other breaches face fines of €15 million or 3% [Source: ev-1].
