Google announced on 2025-03-18 that it had agreed to acquire Wiz, the cloud-security firm, for approximately $32 billion in an all-cash deal — the largest acquisition in Google's history. The deal followed Wiz's rejection of Google's earlier $23 billion offer in July 2024. Wiz co-founder and CEO Assaf Rappaport remained a named principal in the transaction. Google Cloud, under Thomas Kurian, pursued Wiz to close the security gap with AWS and Azure, betting on cloud-security consolidation as the cloud wars' next battleground and on AI-driven cloud adoption as the demand driver. The deal closed in 2025 after shareholder and regulatory steps, with completion reported in July 2025.
