Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz to close its security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, betting that cloud-security consolidation would be the next battleground in the "cloud wars" driven by AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled a major shift toward consolidating the cloud-security market as a critical component of competing for enterprise customers reliant on secure cloud infrastructure.

The acquisition was announced on March 18, 2025, at an approximate value of $32 billion in cash, making it the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This final price followed Wiz’s rejection of an earlier offer from Google worth $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. Although the announcement occurred in early 2025 and the transaction involved shareholder and regulatory steps throughout that year, the deal ultimately closed with completion reported in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].
