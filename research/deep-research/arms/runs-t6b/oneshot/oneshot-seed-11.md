Nvidia’s market value crossed $4 trillion on 2025-07-09, making it the first company in history to close above this threshold [Source: ev-1]. This milestone followed Apple touching $4T intraday on 2025-10-08 [Source: ev-1]. The rapid trajectory of Nvidia's valuation included reaching $1T in mid-2023, $2T in Feb 2024, and $3T in June 2024 before hitting $4T in July 2025 [Source: ev-1].

The primary drivers for this surge were strong demand for Blackwell accelerator GB200/GB300 rack systems, the Stargate project’s ~$500B AI-infrastructure plan, and repeatedly raised capital expenditure guidance from hyperscalers such as Microsoft, Meta, Google, and Amazon throughout 2025 [Source: ev-1]. During this period, Nvidia maintained a dominant share of the AI-accelerator market while its share price crossed about $160 [Source: ev-1]. CEO Jensen Huang characterized this moment as a transition from training to inference demand, describing "AI factories" that sell compute as a commodity [Source: ev-1].

This milestone indicates that the market is pricing AI infrastructure as the largest new capital cycle since the internet era, with Nvidia positioned as the central toll-booth for this spending supercycle [Source: ev-1]. However, it also highlights significant concentration risks, as Nvidia’s weight in the S&P 500 reached record levels and membership in the $4T club has rotated among Nvidia, Apple, and Microsoft through 2025 [Source: ev-1].
