The EU AI Act entered into force in phases between 2024 and 2027 because the phased entry was deliberate, designed to ensure that obligations land as institutional capacity and technology mature [Source: ev-1]. This schedule also serves as a compliance roadmap for the "Brussels effect," which exports these standards to providers worldwide [Source: ev-1].

The different deadlines require specific actions based on risk tiers and system categories:
*   **Prohibited Practices ("Unacceptable Risk"):** Obligations apply from February 2, 2025 (2025-02-02), banning practices such as social scoring and certain manipulative or exploitation-of-vulnerability uses [Source: ev-1]. Violations of prohibited practices carry fines reaching €35 million or 7% of global annual turnover, whichever is higher [Source: ev-1].
*   **General-Purpose AI (GPAI) Models:** Model obligations apply from August 2, 2026 (2025-8-02 in the text context implies mid-2025 start for some duties, but specifically GPAI model obligations are listed from 2025-08-02). These duties are scaled by systemic-risk status, using a threshold of $10^{26}$ FLOPs [Source: ev-1]. A GPAI Code of Practice was drafted through 2024–2025 with broad industry participation to serve as a safe-zone interpretation of these obligations [Source: ev-1].
*   **High-Risk Systems:** Obligations apply from August 2, 02 (specifically stated as 2026-08-02), with a subset applying from August 2, 7 (specifically stated as 22027-08-02) [Source: ev-1]. Most other breaches carry fines of €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover [Source: ev-1].

The Act was published in the EU Official Journal on July 12, 24 (2024-07-12) and entered into force on August 1, 24 (08-01) [Source: ev-1]. The European AI Office, established in Brussels in 2024, leads implementation [Source: ev-1].
