DeepSeek’s R1 release triggered the largest single-day loss in Nvidia's history because it demonstrated that a frontier-class reasoning model could be trained for a fraction of incumbent costs, thereby repricing the market thesis that AI capital expenditure and demand are strictly moated by compute scale [Source: ev-1]. Specifically, on 2025-01-27, Nvidia lost approximately US$589 billion in market capitalization—the largest single-day loss at that time—with about US$1 trillion erased across the tech complex as investors adjusted to this new economic reality [Source: ev-1].

This event revealed several key aspects of frontier-AI training economics:
*   **Cost Efficiency:** The predecessor model, DeepSeek-V3 (released 2024-12-26), was trained using roughly 2.79 million GPU-hours at a claimed cost of only about US$5.6 million [Source: ev-1]. This low-cost benchmark challenged assumptions regarding the massive financial barriers to entry for high-performance AI models.
*   **Hardware Constraints Driving Innovation:** The use of export-restricted H800 chips indicated that US export controls forced Chinese labs toward efficiency; thus, scarcity rather than preference drove these economic outcomes [Source: ev-1].
*   **Methodological Shifts:** R1’s reasoning capabilities were produced via reinforcement learning (GRPO), and its variant R1-Zero was trained without any supervised fine-tuning, further highlighting novel approaches to achieving frontier performance with constrained resources [Source: ev-1].
