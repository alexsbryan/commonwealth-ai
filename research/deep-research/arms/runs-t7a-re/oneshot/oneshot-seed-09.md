Anthropic released the "Claude 4" family, consisting of two models—Opus 4 and Sonnet 4—on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. The decision to launch two distinct models was driven by a specific strategic positioning for each within their pricing tiers. Opus 4 served as the flagship model with higher costs at $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1]. In contrast, Sonnet 4 was positioned as the fast workhorse and developer-default coding model, priced significantly lower at $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that Anthropic viewed agentic coding and computer use as critical competitive fronts in 2025, directly challenging OpenAI’s o-series during an enterprise "agentic-coding gold rush" [Source: ev-1]. By introducing "computer use 2.0," which offered browser control roughly twice as reliable as the previous generation, Anthropic aimed to establish Claude 4 as the safest and most reliable coding agent available [Source: ev-1]. This strategy was validated by record API usage driven by the adoption of Claude Code, a defining product for this market segment [Source: ev-1]. The success of these models ultimately cemented Anthropic's position as a leader in the space, contributing to its raising of a large funding round at a reported $183 billion valuation in late September 2025 [ev-1].
