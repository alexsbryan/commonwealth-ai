Google acquired the cloud-security firm Wiz to close a security gap with competitors AWS and Azure, positioning itself for consolidation in the cloud market driven by AI-driven cloud adoption [Source: ev-1]. The deal signaled that cloud-security consolidation is becoming the next battleground in the "cloud wars" [Source: ev-1].

The acquisition was announced on March 18, 2025, as an all-cash transaction valued at approximately $32 billion, which marked the largest acquisition in Google's history [Source: ev-1]. This final price followed Wiz’s rejection of an earlier offer from Google worth $23 billion in July 2024 [Source: ev-1]. Although the announcement occurred in 2025 and the completion was reported in July 2025 after regulatory steps, the strategic decision to pursue Wiz under Google Cloud leadership (Thomas Kurian) focused specifically on leveraging security capabilities amid rising AI-driven demand for cloud services [Source: ev-1].
