Anthropic released the Claude 4 family as two distinct models, Opus 4 and Sonnet 4, on May 8, 2025 [Source: ev-1]. This dual-model strategy was designed to address different market needs by positioning Opus 4 as the flagship model with pricing of $15 per million input tokens and $75 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1], while positioning Sonnet 4 as a "fast workhorse" and developer-default coding model at lower prices of $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens [Source: ev-1].

The launch signaled that agentic coding had become a defining product category for 2025, driven specifically by the rise of Claude Code [Source: ev-1]. By introducing "computer use 2.0," which was roughly twice as reliable as the previous generation's capabilities, Anthropic positioned itself against competitors like OpenAI’s o-series in what is described as an enterprise agentic-coding gold rush [Source: ev-1]. The release cemented Anthropic's position as a leader in this space, aiming to offer the safest and most reliable coding agent, a strategy that contributed to record API usage and supported a late September 2025 funding round at a reported ~$183 billion valuation [Source: ev-1].
