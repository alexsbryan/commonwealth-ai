# The Saltgrass Ledger

**THE SALTGRASS LEDGER**
*A Tale of Glasswater Stave*

In Glasswater Stave, a salvage port on the Hethering coast, the lock basin holds its water the way the village holds its opinions: behind heavy gates, and grudgingly. So when Corwin Pellow, harbormaster for twenty-one years, a man who wrote everything down and could not be bought, is found floating in the basin at dawn, the whole village has decided by breakfast that it was no accident. Nobody will say so above a whisper.

Maren Ashquill keeps the tide ledger: every opening of the gates, every vessel through them, every height of water against the painted gauge. For eleven years she has watched the harbormaster cross the cobbles at the same hour every morning. Now she starts to wonder what the ledger saw, and why the people she has known all her life are so sure of things they cannot have seen.

Sixteen chapters of salt, tar and quiet lies, in a village where everyone has a reason and the truth comes in with the tide.
