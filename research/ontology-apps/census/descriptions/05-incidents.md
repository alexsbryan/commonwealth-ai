# Production incidents at a software company

I lead our on-call program. The material is the incident chat channels (exported as text), the paging
notifications, and the postmortem documents. Today someone fills in an incident form after the fact.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every incident with its severity, the services it affected, when it started, was detected, was mitigated and was resolved.
2. Who responded to each incident and in what role (incident commander, communications, responder).
3. The timeline of each incident: what was observed or done, when, by whom.
4. The root cause stated in the postmortem.
5. The follow-up actions from each postmortem, with an owner, a due date and whether each is done.
6. Incidents that are the same outage reported twice are one incident.
7. How severity changed during an incident.
