# Contracts at a services firm

I manage our contracts. The material is the signed agreements (as text), their amendments, and the email in
which renewals, notices and amendments are negotiated. Today we keep a contract register in a spreadsheet.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every contract with its counterparty, type (master agreement, statement of work, NDA, lease), and owner on our side.
2. Each contract's effective date, end date, and notice period for renewal or termination.
3. The contract's value and currency, and how an amendment changed it.
4. Which amendments apply to which contract, in order, and what each changed.
5. Which contracts are in force today, and which were in force on a given past date.
6. Which contracts renew automatically and the date by which notice must be given.
7. A statement of work belongs to its master agreement.
