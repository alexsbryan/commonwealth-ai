# Residential lettings for a small landlord

I manage 40 flats. The material is the tenancy agreements (as text), my email with tenants and contractors,
and contractor invoices.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every tenancy: which flat, which tenants, start and end date, monthly rent and currency, deposit held.
2. Rent changes over time, and when each took effect.
3. Renewals and notices to quit, with their dates.
4. Every maintenance request: which flat, what was wrong, when it was reported, who fixed it, its status and cost.
5. Which flats are empty and since when.
6. A tenant who moves to another of my flats is the same person with a new tenancy.
