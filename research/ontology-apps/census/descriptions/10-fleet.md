# A delivery company's vans

I look after 25 vans. The material is the drivers' daily check emails (mileage, defects), garage job sheets and
invoices (as text), and fuel receipts.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every van, by registration, with its model and year.
2. Each van's mileage over time, from the readings drivers and garages report.
3. Every defect reported, on which van, by which driver, and whether and when it was fixed.
4. Every service and repair: which van, which garage, the work done, the cost and currency, the date.
5. When each van's next service is due by mileage or by date.
6. Which driver had which van on which day.
