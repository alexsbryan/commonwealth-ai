# Employee expenses at a consultancy

I run finance. The material is the expense-claim emails with receipts (as text), the approval replies from
managers, and the payment notices from our bank.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every expense claim: who claimed, for what, the amount and currency, the date incurred, the category, the client project if any.
2. Who approved or rejected each claim, and when.
3. Each claim's status (submitted, approved, rejected, paid) and the payment date.
4. Totals per employee, per project and per category for a period.
5. Claims that break policy (over the limit for their category, or missing a receipt).
6. Claims re-submitted after a rejection are the same claim.
