# Company policies

I keep our internal policies (travel, security, leave, expenses and so on). The material is the policy memos as
issued, the leadership-meeting minutes where policies are approved, amended or retired, and the announcement emails.
Today a wiki page lists "current policies" and is usually out of date.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every policy, with its owner and the area it governs.
2. Which version of each policy is in force now, and which was in force on a given past date.
3. When and by whom each policy was approved, amended or retired.
4. What each amendment changed (the clause before and after).
5. Which policy replaced a retired one.
6. Every rule a policy imposes (who must or must not do what) with the clause it comes from.
