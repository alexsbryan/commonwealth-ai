# Grants at a small charity

I handle our grant funding. The material is our correspondence with funders, the application documents we
submit, award letters, and the reports we send back.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every application: which funder, which programme, how much we asked for and in what currency, when we applied.
2. The outcome of each application (pending, awarded, declined, withdrawn), and the amount awarded.
3. The conditions of each award, and every reporting deadline it imposes.
4. Which reports we have sent and which are overdue.
5. Payments received against each award, with dates and amounts.
6. Our contact at each funder.
