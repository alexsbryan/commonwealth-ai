# Hiring at a 60-person company

I run recruiting. The material is my recruiting inbox (candidates, agencies, hiring managers) and the interview
notes interviewers paste into email after each interview. Today I keep a spreadsheet by hand: one row per
candidate per opening.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. For each opening, which candidates are in play and at what stage (applied, screen, onsite, offer, hired, rejected, withdrew)?
2. Who referred or sourced each candidate (an employee, an agency, or the candidate applied directly)?
3. Who interviewed a candidate, for which opening, on what date, and what each interviewer recommended?
4. What we offered (base salary, currency, start date) and whether the offer was accepted, declined or revised.
5. When a candidate moved stage, and on whose say-so.
6. Which agencies are owed a fee for a hire, and how much.
7. A candidate who applies to two openings is one person with two applications.
