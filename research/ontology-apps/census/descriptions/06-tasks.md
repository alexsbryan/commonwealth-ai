# A small product team's work

I manage a seven-person team. The material is our meeting notes and the team chat. Work is agreed in
conversation; nobody keeps the tracker up to date.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every task we agreed to do, with its owner and due date.
2. Each task's status (open, in progress, blocked, done, dropped) and when it last changed.
3. Which tasks block which other tasks.
4. Who asked for each task and why.
5. Tasks that were reassigned, and to whom.
6. Which tasks belong to which project or milestone.
