# Purchasing at a manufacturing site

I run purchasing. The material is the purchase-request emails, the approval replies, purchase orders sent to
suppliers, supplier confirmations and invoices (as text). Today we track it in a shared spreadsheet and miss things.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every purchase request: who asked, for what, how many, estimated cost and currency.
2. Who approved or rejected each request, and when; requests above 10,000 need two approvals.
3. The purchase order a request became, its supplier and its total.
4. The invoices against each purchase order and whether the invoiced amount matches the order.
5. Each order's status (requested, approved, ordered, delivered, invoiced, paid, cancelled).
6. Our suppliers, with their contacts, and how much we have ordered from each this year.
