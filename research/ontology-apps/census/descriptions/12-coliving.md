# A 20-person coliving house

I am one of twenty people sharing a house with a big shared kitchen. The material is the house group chat
(exported as text) where we propose and agree house rules, assign chores, and split shared purchases.

What I need to be able to ask:
1. The house rules in force now, and when each was agreed.
2. Rules that were proposed but not agreed, and who objected.
3. How a rule changed when it was amended, and which rules were dropped.
4. The chore rota: who has which chore in which week, and whether it got done.
5. Shared purchases: who paid, how much and in what currency, and who owes whom.
6. A rule, a chore or a purchase mentioned several times in the chat is one thing.
