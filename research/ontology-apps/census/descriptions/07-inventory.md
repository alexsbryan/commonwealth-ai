# Spare parts at three warehouses

I manage spare parts for our field technicians across three warehouses. The material is the warehouse emails
(receipts, transfers between warehouses, issues to technicians) and the monthly stock-take sheets (as text).

What I need to be able to ask:
1. Every part, with its part number, description and unit of measure.
2. How many of each part each warehouse holds, as of the last stock-take and as of the latest movement.
3. Every movement: a receipt from a supplier, a transfer between warehouses, an issue to a technician, with quantity and date.
4. Where a stock-take count disagreed with what the movements imply.
5. Which technician holds which issued parts.
6. Parts below their reorder level.
