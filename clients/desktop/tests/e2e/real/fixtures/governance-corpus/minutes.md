# House Meeting Minutes

Decisions recorded here amend the charter. The most recent decision on a topic
is the one in force.

## Decision — February 10, 2026

Quiet hours will begin at 10 PM on weeknights (Sunday through Thursday), earlier
than the charter's 11 PM, because several residents start work early. Weekend
quiet hours are unchanged.

## Decision — March 14, 2026

No overnight guests are permitted for the remainder of the winter while the
heating is being repaired, notwithstanding the charter's two-night allowance.

## Decision — March 28, 2026

Guests may park on the street in front of the house. They should not use the
residents' driveway spaces.

## Decision — April 2, 2026

The resident who cooks the main house dinner is excused from kitchen cleanup
that night; the cleanup rotates to the next person on the chore wheel.
