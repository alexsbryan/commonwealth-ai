# Maple House Charter

Maple House is a co-living community of about twenty residents who share a
kitchen, common rooms, and the responsibility for keeping the house livable.
This charter records the founding agreements. Later decisions, recorded in the
meeting minutes, amend it over time.

## Article I — Guests

Residents may host guests. A guest may stay up to two nights in any week.
Hosts are responsible for their guests' conduct in the common areas.

## Article II — Quiet hours

Quiet hours begin at 11 PM every night and end at 7 AM. During quiet hours,
residents keep noise out of the shared walls and common rooms.

## Article III — The kitchen

The kitchen is shared. Whoever cooks cleans the kitchen afterward, including
the stove, the counters, and any shared pots used.

## Amendment process

Any resident may propose a change at the weekly house meeting. A decision
recorded in the minutes supersedes the charter where the two conflict.
