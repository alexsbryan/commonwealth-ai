# Elowen Marsh, Keeper of the Meridian Light

Elowen Marsh served as head keeper of the Meridian Lighthouse from
1903 to 1931, the longest tenure of any keeper in the station's
history. She was the first woman appointed head keeper on the Cape
Vesper coast.

Her logbooks, preserved in the Hollow Sound Archive, record 4,212
consecutive nights of operation without a single lapse of the light.

Marsh is best remembered for the rescue of the schooner Tamarind on
the night of 2 November 1912. When the Tamarind lost her rudder in a
gale and drifted toward the Vesper shoals, Marsh launched the
station's surfboat with her assistant and brought all nine of the
schooner's crew ashore. She was awarded the Silver Compass Medal for
the rescue in 1913.
