Field Notes — Survey of the Salt Garden Tide Pools
Expedition of the research vessel Periwinkle, April 2024

Day 1. We anchored the Periwinkle off the Salt Garden at 06:40 and
took the skiff in at low tide. The pools cover roughly three hectares
of terraced volcanic rock. Water temperature in the upper pools
measured 14.2 degrees Celsius.

Day 2. Counted 312 individuals of the violet anemone (our provisional
name: Actinia violacea pallida) across the eastern terraces. The
largest specimen measured 11 centimeters across the oral disc. Dr.
Ferro believes the population is the largest recorded north of the
Mirren Islands.

Day 3. Heavy rain. We sheltered in the wheelhouse and catalogued
samples. The expedition's photographer, Naomi Aoki, developed the
first plates of the bioluminescent shrimp observed on night one;
their blue-green pulse repeats every four seconds, which Dr. Ferro
suspects is a synchronized mating display.

Day 4. Final transect. Total species logged for the expedition: 87,
of which 6 are candidate new records for the region. We weighed
anchor at dusk. The Salt Garden deserves a full season's study; four
days barely scratched its surface.
